## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with full-stack range and AI-product experience.*

Reading ~60 full Israeli job descriptions across two runs today, three claims decide these roles. Put them in the first third of the CV and the first line of every outreach message:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100,000+ requests/day.** The single most differentiating line in the profile. It signals distributed-systems and cloud-native depth that the "2–3 years" label actively hides. It is what makes AppsFlyer, Zero Networks, Via, Questar and Cardo plausible — and AppsFlyer's req literally asks for "resilient services using Kubernetes."
2. **Production applications processing 100,000+ documents/day.** Reframes seniority as *system scale* rather than *years served* — the argument that closes the gap at Shopic and Cardo.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement.** This has now fully crossed from differentiator to table stakes in the Israeli market: AppsFlyer, Fireblocks, PANGO, Moon Active, Workiz, Questar, Shopic, Zero Networks, Cymulate, DriveNets and WalkMe all named AI/LLM tooling in their reqs this run. The candidate is genuinely ahead of the curve. **Be concrete** — say RAG, evaluation pipelines, LLM APIs, agent tooling, not "AI experience."

Support with **MSc + BSc in CS, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** — a B.Sc. in CS was a hard requirement at AppsFlyer, Via, ERGO NEXT and Zero Networks — plus **root-cause analysis and production debugging**, which appeared as an explicit requirement in the majority of the roles that survived filtering.

**De-emphasize:**
* **Do not present as Senior.** Every 5+ posting read today wanted two prior positions or leadership scope. Applying up burns the pipeline rather than widening it — and this run's data shows the mid-level band is where the real openings are.
* **Do not lead with the vocational-training / teaching work.** It reads as a parallel career and dilutes the 2–3 years of full-time engineering. Reposition it late and reframe it as *mentoring, technical communication and code review* — AppsFlyer's req explicitly asks the engineer to "mentor, advise, and work collaboratively."
* **Do not lead with ML/data-science research.** It attracts pure-research and MLOps roles that are out of scope. Frame the AI work as *AI-enabled product and platform engineering*.
* **Correct the Indeed profile.** `get_resume` still shows **"Minimum Salary: GBP 2,000"** — a GBP figure on an Israeli search is a data-entry artifact that will misroute matching. The profile also lists the current role's employer field as "ChatGPT, PaLM, AWS, S3, Docker" rather than a company name, which reads as broken to any recruiter who opens it. Both are worth ten minutes at profile.indeed.com.
