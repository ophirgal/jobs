## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services — with full-stack range and production AI-product experience.*

Three runs and ~110 full Israeli job descriptions later, the evidence on what decides these roles is consistent enough to state as a rule. Put these three claims in the first third of the CV and the first line of every outreach message:

1. **"Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day."** This is the highest-leverage sentence in the profile. It answers the Kubernetes requirement, the distributed-systems requirement and the production-scale requirement simultaneously, and it is concrete enough that no interviewer mistakes it for résumé inflation. It appeared as a direct hit against AppsFlyer, Via, Cardo, Questar, Zero Networks and Cato's AI Platform req.
2. **"Go and Python in production."** Roughly every backend req in this market opens with a language line. Naming both — with Go first — clears that gate at Descope, AppsFlyer, Via and Cato in a single stroke.
3. **"Built and evaluated production generative-AI systems, and shipped internal engineering tools independently."** This is what separated the candidate from generic mid-level backend applicants in *five* of this run's roles (Descope, both Cato reqs, Fireblocks, Lemonade). The Israeli market in August 2026 is pricing AI-adjacent backend experience at a premium, and a growing number of postings — Lemonade's among them — now make AI-tool fluency an explicit requirement rather than a bonus.

**What to de-emphasize:**
* **Do not lead with "Full-Stack Engineer."** It invites comparison against frontend-heavy candidates and, as Descope's own full-stack req demonstrates ("significantly more Frontend work than Backend"), those roles sit further from the candidate's strengths *and* ask for more years. React and TypeScript should appear as range that de-risks a backend hire, not as the headline.
* **Do not foreground the teaching years.** They are genuine evidence of communication and mentoring ability — and they are a real differentiator for exactly two roles here (Cato's Agentic AI req, which explicitly wants someone who can work with non-technical stakeholders, and Fireblocks PS). Everywhere else, prominent teaching history invites an interviewer to mentally discount the professional engineering timeline, which is the opposite of what a 2–3-year candidate needs.
* **Do not present the ML/data-science research background as a specialism.** It supports the AI-product story; it should not suggest a research track. Every pure-research and algorithm role in this market wants a PhD or a publication record, and drifting toward that framing competes in the one arena where the profile is genuinely weak.
* **Do not describe the profile as senior.** The market punishes this. Titles here run 2–3 years ahead of their stated requirements in both directions — Descope's 3-year "Senior" req and Cato's 5-year plain "Software Engineer" req are the same phenomenon — so the candidate should compete on stated requirements and demonstrated scale, never on title.
