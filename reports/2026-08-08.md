## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with full-stack range and AI-product experience.* Every high-tier role in this report was won or lost on three specific claims, so put them in the first third of the CV and the first line of every outreach message:

It signals distributed-systems and cloud-native depth that the "2–3 years" label actively hides, and it is what makes Zero Networks, Questar, Cardo and Google plausible.
2. **Production applications processing 100,000+ documents/day** — reframes seniority in terms of *system scale* instead of *years*, which is the argument that closes the gap at Shopic and Cardo.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement** — nearly every Israeli posting found this run (PANGO, Lemonade, Workiz, Questar, Shopic, Zero Networks, Google) now explicitly asks for LLM/AI-tooling fluency. This has shifted from differentiator to table stakes in this market, and the candidate is genuinely ahead of the curve on it. Say so concretely.

Support with: **MSc + BSc in CS, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** (this is what unlocks Google's advanced-degree path and satisfies the BSc-mandatory reqs at ERGO NEXT, Lemonade, Zero Networks, Align, and Moon Active), plus **root-cause analysis and production debugging**, which appeared as an explicit requirement in six of the eleven listed roles.

**De-emphasize:**
* **Do not present as Senior.** Nothing in this profile supports Senior/Staff/Lead framing, and every 5+ year posting I read this run wanted 2+ prior positions or leadership scope. Applying up will burn the pipeline rather than expand it.
* **Do not lead with the vocational-training / teaching-instructor work.** It reads as a parallel career and risks diluting the 2–3 years of full-time engineering. Keep it, but reposition it late and reframe it as *mentoring, technical communication and code review* — which several postings (Workiz, ERGO NEXT, Zero Networks) do ask for.
* **Do not lead with ML/data-science research.** It attracts pure-research and MLOps roles that are explicitly out of scope. Frame the AI experience as *AI-enabled product and platform engineering*, not model research.
* **Drop the Indeed profile's "Minimum Salary: GBP 2,000"** — a GBP figure on an Israeli search is a data-entry artifact that will misroute matching. Worth correcting at profile.indeed.com.
