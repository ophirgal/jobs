## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed services, with production AI-system experience.** Three concrete anchors, in this order, and they should appear in the first four lines of any CV or outreach message:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day** — this is the single most differentiating line in the profile. It is systems ownership at scale, not feature work, and it is exactly what the Kubernetes-and-cloud reqs (AppsFlyer, Zero Networks, Nanit, Cato AI Platform) are buying.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** — the throughput number that makes "distributed systems" concrete rather than aspirational, and the bridge into every data-platform and crawling role here.
3. **Building and evaluating production generative-AI systems** — no longer a differentiator but now table stakes: it appears as an explicit requirement or bonus in *six* of these twelve postings (Cato ×2, Descope, Fireblocks, AppsFlyer, Nanit MLOps). Frame it as *shipping and evaluating AI in production*, never as research.

Support with: **MSc + BSc CS, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** — Israeli employers weigh degree pedigree and GPA heavily, and Lendbuzz scores it explicitly. Add **PostgreSQL, AWS, TypeScript/React** as the secondary layer that makes full-stack roles reachable without diluting the backend identity.

**De-emphasize:** the generic "full-stack developer" framing — it competes against a large, cheaper pool and hides the distributed-systems work that is this candidate's actual edge; frontend/React depth beyond a one-line mention; **freelance teaching years, which must never be presented as professional engineering tenure** — surface them instead as evidence of mentoring and technical communication, which is precisely the framing Cato's non-technical-stakeholder requirement rewards; and academic ML/data-science research, which invites mis-slotting into research reqs that explicitly want a PhD.
