## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and high-throughput cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.**

Concretely, the CV and every outreach message should open on the three assets that are rare in combination at this seniority:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day** — this is a platform-engineering credential most engineers with 2–3 years do not have, and it is the single line that makes 3+ and even 4+ postings reachable.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** — concrete throughput evidence, which converts "scalable systems" from a claim into a number.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — nearly every posting in this report now asks for AI-tooling fluency, and most candidates can only claim they *use* Cursor. Being able to say the work was *evaluating and improving* AI system quality is a genuine differentiator, and it is what makes the Cato, Fireblocks, At-Bay and Nanit MLOps roles credible rather than aspirational.

Add **full-stack range (React, TypeScript)** as a secondary capability, framed as "backend engineer who can own a feature end-to-end" — never as a primary identity.

**De-emphasize:** freelance and vocational teaching years (they are a communication and mentoring signal, not engineering tenure, and listing them prominently invites the reader to discount the whole timeline); academic ML/data-science research as a headline (it reads as a research-scientist profile and works against the hands-on product-engineering framing — keep it as a supporting line for the AI-adjacent roles only); and the total-years figure. Lead every document with **scope and throughput**, not duration.
