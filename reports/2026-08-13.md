## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specialising in Go and Python, Kubernetes, and high-throughput distributed cloud services — with full-stack range and production AI-system experience.**

Concretely, the CV and every outreach message should open on three artefacts, in this order: (1) Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day; (2) production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day; (3) evaluation and improvement of generative-AI systems in production. That sequence establishes infrastructure depth, scale, and AI relevance before any recruiter reaches the years-of-experience line — which matters, because the market is currently asking for 4+ and the candidate has 2–3.

The MSc in Computer Science (3.9 GPA, University of Maryland) should be visible early rather than buried in an education footer: several roles this run make a B.Sc. a hard must, and at least one (Unframe, N2) explicitly counts research experience toward the experience bar.

**De-emphasise:** the freelance and vocational teaching years — they are a real asset for communication and mentoring, but listing them adjacent to engineering roles invites a recruiter to read the timeline as thinner than it is. Keep them in a separate section, framed as mentoring and technical communication. Also de-emphasise generic "full-stack developer" framing, pure frontend/CSS work, and any positioning that reads as data science or research — the target here is production backend engineering, and the AI experience should be presented as *AI systems engineering and evaluation*, not as ML research.
