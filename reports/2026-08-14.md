## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-scale distributed cloud services, with full-stack delivery in TypeScript/React and production generative-AI experience.**

Concretely, the three artefacts that should appear in the first six lines of every application:
1. **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway** — this is the strongest single line on the CV. It reads as platform/infrastructure engineering with real distributed-systems ownership, and it is what makes rows #3–#8 land.
2. **Production systems at 100k+ requests/day and 100k+ documents/day** — the throughput number does the work that a year-count cannot.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — this is now a named, mandatory-adjacent requirement in four of the eighteen roles (#1, #2, #11, #17), not a decoration.

**De-emphasize:** the freelance/vocational teaching years — they must never sit where a reader could count them as engineering tenure, and where the CV is scanned for "years of experience" the answer must resolve cleanly to 2–3 years of full-time SWE. Also de-emphasize pure frontend framing (CSS, UI implementation), academic ML research as a headline (it belongs as supporting evidence under the AI-systems line, not as an identity — it invites data-scientist screening the candidate would lose), and the breadth-over-depth "generic full-stack developer" label, which competes against a much larger and cheaper applicant pool.

**On seniority:** apply as a mid-level Software Engineer. Do not self-title Senior. Two rows here (#6 Descope, and the "Senior" framing at several others) carry a Senior label against a 3+ year bar — the correct move is to meet the stated numeric requirement and let the company own the title question, not to pre-inflate.
