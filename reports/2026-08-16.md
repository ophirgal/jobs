2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** at Qualtrics, with React/TypeScript full-stack ownership — this covers the product-engineering and full-stack roles (Voyantis, Innovid, SOURCIX, Minute Media, shopic).
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation in production, plus an MSc and prior ML research** — this is genuinely rare among backend applicants and is the deciding differentiator at Red Hat, Upwind AI, Guidde, Unframe, At-Bay and the Google SOAR roles with GenAI mandates.

**De-emphasise:**
* **Do not lead with "full-stack developer."** It flattens the backend depth into a generic profile and invites comparison with a much larger, weaker applicant pool.
* **Do not present the vocational teaching as engineering experience.** Position it where it is genuinely strong — mentoring, communication, technical explanation — and keep the professional-experience count clean at ~2–3 years. Conflating the two is the fastest way to lose credibility with an Israeli technical recruiter.
* **Do not over-index on the AI/ML research background** when applying to backend platform roles. It is a differentiator, not the headline; leading with it risks being redirected toward data-science roles the brief explicitly excludes.
* **Do not apologise for the years gap on 3+ and 4+ postings.** State the scope achieved instead — 100k+ requests/day, 100k+ documents/day, independently-owned internal tooling — and let the evidence do the work.
