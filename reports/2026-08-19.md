## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed systems, with full-stack TypeScript/React delivery and production generative-AI experience.**

Three concrete numbers should appear in the first third of the CV and in every outreach message, because they are what substitute for tenure: **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day**, **production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day**, and the **MSc in Computer Science from the University of Maryland**. The MSc is not decoration in this batch — it is a hard qualification at Google (both new reqs) and at Red Hat, where it substitutes outright for the experience minimum.

Against 4+ year postings, do not argue tenure. Argue scope: the systems already run at the scale the posting describes.

**De-emphasise:** freelance and vocational teaching as a *duration* of engineering experience — it belongs under communication, mentoring and leadership, never in the years-of-experience count where it invites a credibility challenge. Also de-emphasise pure frontend/CSS depth (it draws exactly the wrong roles), academic ML research framed as research rather than as shipped production capability, and any positioning that reads as generic "full-stack developer" — that framing competes against a very crowded Israeli junior pool and hides the differentiator.
