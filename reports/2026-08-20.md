# 🎯 Israel Backend / Full-Stack Job Report — 2026-08-20

**Run 15.** Every role below was retrieved live in this run — via the Indeed connector, or a successful fetch of the posting or its live job board. Nothing is reconstructed from memory or from a guessed URL.

**Headline: 6 new roles, 38 re-verified as still open.**

The single best find in weeks is **Lendbuzz — Backend Engineer (Tel Aviv)**, whose written requirement is *"1–3 years of hands-on experience in backend development"* with **Python and PostgreSQL** as the core stack — and which explicitly rewards a *"Top-tier university degree with a GPA of 90+ / Graduated with honors."* The candidate lands inside the experience band rather than arguing past it, matches the primary language, and carries a 3.9 GPA from Maryland at both BSc and MSc level. Nothing else found this run converts as cleanly.

The wider market picture is unchanged from yesterday and worth stating plainly: **Israeli mid-level backend inventory is thin and the new supply skews senior.** Twenty-one otherwise-attractive postings were fetched in full, read, and rejected this run — almost all on a 5-, 6- or 7-year experience floor, a hard Hebrew requirement, or an embedded/C++ core. They are itemised by name under Search Gaps so the rejections are auditable rather than implied.

Two roles previously dropped as unverifiable — **Google SWE III, Full Stack, SOAR Pulsar** and **Minute Media — Full Stack Developer** — reappeared in live inventory today and are restored to the still-open table, along with **shopic**. That is a useful reminder that a single day's absence from an aggregator is not proof of closure.
