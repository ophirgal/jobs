The one new role that survived is a good one: **Google — Software Engineer III, Filestore (Tel Aviv)**, posted yesterday, whose minimum qualification reads *"2 years of experience with software development … or 1 year of experience with an advanced degree."* The MSc converts that into a requirement the candidate clears outright, and the preferred column asks for a Master's or PhD in CS plus distributed-systems knowledge.

The strongest role overall remains **Lendbuzz — Backend Engineer**, re-verified live today and still stating **"1–3 years of hands-on experience in backend development"** with Python and PostgreSQL. Nothing found since has displaced it.

### Google — Software Engineer III, Filestore · Tel Aviv · hybrid

**Verified this run** via the Indeed connector, full job description retrieved (`JOBSEARCH_2138` / `JOBSEARCH_2193`, posted **August 20, 2026** — one day old).

**Minimum qualifications, verbatim:**
> "Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience. **2 years of experience with software development in one or more programming languages, or 1 year of experience with an advanced degree.**"

**Preferred qualifications, verbatim:**
> "**Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science** or related technical field. 2 years of experience with data structures and algorithms. Software development experience in Java. Knowledge of clustered/distributed and concurrent programming. Familiarity with cloud computing concepts and technologies, and **knowledge of filesystems and storage**. Good understanding of distributed systems principles and concepts."

**Why it fits:** The minimum has an explicit advanced-degree path — one year of industry experience with an MSc — and the candidate brings two to three years *plus* the MSc, clearing the bar rather than arguing against it. The role is *"development of new features, infrastructure, and performance improvements in a complex distributed and performance sensitive system"*, which is the SAP work restated: Kubernetes controllers behind a globally distributed API gateway serving 100,000+ requests/day is exactly complex, distributed and performance-sensitive. The Master's in CS sits in the preferred column, so the strongest academic credential is scored rather than ignored.

**Main gap / risk:** Java is the named preferred language and is absent from the profile; Go and Python are not mentioned. Filesystems and storage are a genuine domain gap — this is Google Cloud's managed NFS product, and the preferred quals want storage knowledge specifically. Google's SWE loop is algorithms-first regardless of fit, and the posting states *"this position requires in-person interviews as part of the hiring process."*

**Probability: High** (on written requirements) — tempered in practice by Google's interview bar, which is the real gate here, not the fit.

## 🔹 Positioning recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python over Kubernetes and AWS, with production distributed-systems ownership and applied generative-AI experience.**

Three concrete artefacts should carry every application:
- **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100,000+ requests/day** — this is the single strongest line, and it is what makes SWE III and "4+ years" postings arguable rather than hopeless.
- **Production pipelines processing 100,000+ documents/day at Qualtrics** — the throughput number that answers "have you worked at scale?" without invoking tenure.
- **The MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9)** — not as a credential but as a *requirement-clearing device*: three of the Google reqs and Red Hat's posting have advanced-degree minimum paths that turn the degree into a year of experience.

Also foreground: root-cause analysis and production debugging (named as a duty in the shopic, 4M Analytics and Similarweb-class postings), and generative-AI system evaluation (which is genuinely differentiating in the current Israeli market, where most AI reqs want production LLM work).

**De-emphasise:** the freelance teaching years as *engineering* tenure — quantify professional experience as two-to-three years and let the teaching stand separately as evidence of communication and mentoring. Also de-emphasise pure frontend/CSS depth (it invites frontend-role routing), and avoid framing the profile as "generalist full-stack developer" — the market is full of those, and the Go/Kubernetes/distributed-systems angle is what separates this candidate from them.
