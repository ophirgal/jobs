## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with a single sentence, everywhere:

> **Backend engineer with production Go, Python and Kubernetes at scale — Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day at SAP, and document-processing systems handling 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics — with full-stack TypeScript/React range and hands-on generative-AI system evaluation.**

**Emphasise, in this order:**
1. **Go + Kubernetes together.** This is the scarcest thing on the CV in the Israeli market and it is what separates the candidate from the large pool of Python-only backend applicants. It carried three of the top six roles on this list.
2. **Throughput numbers, always quantified.** 100k+ req/day and 100k+ docs/day. Every high-tier posting this run asked for scale; numbers are the cheapest way to answer that.
3. **Independent ownership of internal engineering tooling.** Two roles on this list (AppDome, Guidde) are *entirely* this, and it appeared as a stated responsibility in Zero Networks and Balance as well.
4. **Generative-AI system evaluation as an engineering discipline** — not as research. Every AI-adjacent posting found this run wanted production AI engineering, evals and observability, not modelling.
5. **MSc in CS with a 3.9 GPA** — this is a real screening asset in Israel and is explicitly named as preferred in the Google and Lendbuzz postings.

**De-emphasise:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Present them as evidence of communication and mentoring, never in the professional-experience count. Conflating them invites a credibility problem in a market where "2–3 years" is checked carefully.
* **The academic ML / data-science research background as a headline.** It pulls the profile toward data-science and research-scientist roles that are explicitly out of scope. Keep it as a supporting line under the generative-AI work.
* **Frontend breadth as a primary identity.** "Full-stack developer" positioning competes against a very crowded Israeli pool and undersells the Kubernetes/Go differentiator. Frontend is range, not the pitch.
* **Java.** Listing it prominently pulls the profile toward Java-primary roles (Nanox AI, Cato, Thales) where the candidate is genuinely weaker than the applicant pool.
