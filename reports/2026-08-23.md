## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Present as a **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production experience in AI-enabled products.** Not as a generic full-stack developer — that framing puts the candidate in the largest and least differentiated applicant pool in Israel.

**Lead with, in this order:**
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day** — the single most differentiating item on the CV. It is concrete, high-scale, infrastructure-level, and very few candidates at 2–3 years have written a controller.
2. **Go and Python backend development in production** — Go is scarce at this experience level in Israel and appears in Stream.Security, Upwind, Cato, Radware and Google SecOps.
3. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics** — throughput evidence, and the bridge into data-platform and AI-infrastructure roles.
4. **Generative-AI system evaluation** — now a named requirement (BioCatch, Voyantis, Zoomd, Radware, Guidde, CodeValue), not a curiosity.
5. **MSc, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** — carries real weight at Google, Red Hat and multinational R&D centres.

**De-emphasise:** the freelance/vocational teaching years — never let them read as a substitute for full-time engineering experience, since that invites a mid-level candidate to be mis-screened. Compress them into a single line evidencing **mentoring and technical communication**. Also de-emphasise pure frontend and CSS work, and the academic ML/data-science research, except where a posting explicitly asks for AI experience — for backend roles it dilutes the systems narrative.

**On the years question:** several strong roles say "3+" or "4+" against 2–3 held. Do not pad. Instead, answer the years bar with **scope evidence** — controller development, 100k+ req/day, 100k+ docs/day — which is what actually moves an Israeli hiring manager past a numeric filter.
