## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed services — with full-stack range in TypeScript/React and production experience building AI-enabled systems.*

Concretely, the three artefacts that should appear in the first six lines of the CV and in every outreach message:

1. **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — this is the single most differentiating item and maps onto rows 1, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 16.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — the scale credential that substitutes for tenure when a posting asks for 4–5 years.
3. **Evaluating and improving generative-AI systems** — now a *stated requirement* rather than a bonus at Google SecOps (#5), Voyantis (#11), Guidde (#14), Lemonade (#12) and CodeValue (#4). This has shifted from differentiator to table stakes in the Israeli market and should be surfaced accordingly.

Add explicitly, because screeners will not infer it: **"2+ years hands-on Kubernetes"** as its own line. Stream.Security (#1) gates on exactly that phrasing and it is currently implied rather than stated.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance/vocational teaching** — keep it as a one-line signal of mentoring and communication, but never adjacent to the professional-experience total, where it invites a seniority misread in either direction.
- **Academic ML/data-science research** — it pulls toward research-scientist screening, which is an explicit exclusion. Frame the AI work as *production systems and evaluation*, not modelling.
- **Frontend depth as a headline.** React/TypeScript is range, not identity. Leading with it costs backend credibility on rows 1, 5, 6, 7 and 9 — the highest-probability roles in the file.
- **Breadth-first tech lists.** A long flat skills block dilutes the Go/Kubernetes signal that is doing the actual work here.
