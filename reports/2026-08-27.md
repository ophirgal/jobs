## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer with Go, Python, and Kubernetes at production scale — full-stack capable, with generative-AI product experience.**

Three concrete assets for the first five lines of any application:

1. **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day.** The differentiating credential. It reads as platform/infrastructure engineering well above two years, and it is what makes #1, #3, #4, #6 and #7 winnable.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day.** The throughput number does the arguing on any "high-scale" or "distributed systems" requirement.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement.** Now a first-class requirement rather than a curiosity — it appears in #5, #9, #12 and #14. Pair it with the text-analytics, classification and summarization background.

Add the **MSc in Computer Science (3.9 GPA, University of Maryland)** wherever a posting names an advanced degree as preferred — at Google it substitutes directly for required experience, and at Lendbuzz and NEXT it satisfies a stated degree requirement outright.

**De-emphasise:** the freelance/vocational **teaching years**. They are genuine evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth, and belong in the interview and in a short CV line — but never presented in a way that implies 4–5 years of full-time engineering. An Israeli hiring manager who spots inflated tenure will discount everything else on the page. Also de-emphasise **pure frontend framing**: React and TypeScript belong as *supporting* skills under a backend headline. Finally, avoid **Senior/Staff/Lead** self-description — a strong mid-level engineer with unusually good infrastructure evidence is a more persuasive story than an inflated one.

**One additional item to settle:** the Indeed profile currently reads *"Willing to Relocate: No"* and lists the most recent roles ambiguously (job titles appear in the employer field). If the U.S. market is genuinely of interest, that profile needs correcting — several U.S. sources rank on it, and as written it will suppress exactly the NYC/DC matches this report was asked to find.
