## 🔹 Positioning recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with AI-product engineering experience.** The three assets that carry the most weight, in order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day.** This is the differentiator. Most engineers with 2–3 years have deployed *to* Kubernetes; building controllers *on* the Kubernetes API is a materially rarer skill and it satisfies the hard Kubernetes requirements at Stream.Security, Zero Networks, Upwind and AppDome outright.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** Concrete throughput evidence for every "high-scale" and "distributed systems" requirement in the table.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation plus text analytics, classification and summarization.** This is what turns the candidate from qualified into differentiated at NYT, Jeen.ai, Voyantis and the Google SOAR/SecOps AI workstreams.

Add **MSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland (3.9 GPA)** prominently for the Google applications specifically, where it is a named preferred qualification.

**De-emphasize:** freelance and vocational teaching — it should appear as evidence of communication and mentoring ability, never positioned as engineering tenure, and never in a way that reads as padding 2–3 years into more. Also de-emphasize pure frontend and CSS/UI work (it invites misfiling into frontend roles), DevOps-flavored framing (the target is software engineering, not operations), and academic ML research framing, which pulls toward data-science roles that are explicitly out of scope. Do not present as Senior — every High-probability role here is mid-level, and self-titling upward is the fastest way to fail a screen.

**New York City — searched, one role included.** Indeed connector across backend/Go/Kubernetes/Python/AI-platform keyword sets, plus Built In and Greenhouse. The market produced volume but almost entirely at Senior and Staff level: Ramp, Rippling, Hex, LangChain, Cloaked, Gecko Robotics, Squarespace, Fora, BlackRock — all Senior/Staff/VP and all failing the experience gate before the technical gate was even reached. It was excluded because two stated requirements are unmet, not merely preferred: hands-on experience designing or operating time-series/columnar storage systems (ClickHouse, TimescaleDB, Druid, InfluxDB), and streaming/data-processing experience (Kafka, Arrow, Flink, Spark). Noting it here rather than silently dropping it, because it is the closest U.S. near-miss of the run and may become a fit later.
