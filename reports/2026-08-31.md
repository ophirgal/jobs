## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services — with production experience shipping and evaluating AI-enabled features.**

Three concrete anchors, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day** (SAP). The rarest thing on the résumé — controller development against the K8s API, not deployment onto Kubernetes — and a mandatory requirement at #1, #7, #10 and #12.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics). Use this whenever a posting says "high-volume", "pipeline", "ingestion" or "at scale".
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** The differentiator at #4, #5 and #13, and what separates the candidate from other 2–3 year backend applicants. Say *evaluation frameworks and production integration*, not "worked with LLMs".

**One adjustment for this week's new roles:** at NEXT Insurance (#3, #14) neither Go nor Kubernetes is in the job description. There, lead instead with **Python in production, AWS distributed systems, relational-database and query-optimization work, and end-to-end feature ownership with high test coverage** — and treat the **B.Sc./MSc in Computer Science** as a headline item, since a CS degree is a stated hard requirement rather than a preference.

Support with: full-stack ownership in React/TypeScript (a genuine plus at #1 and #13 — never the headline), the **MSc in Computer Science** (an explicit preferred qualification at both Google requisitions), and root-cause analysis / debugging.

**De-emphasize:** the freelance teaching years as *engineering* experience — surface them only as evidence of communication and mentoring, never in the years-of-experience count; frontend framework depth; any suggestion of architect, lead or management ambition (the mid-level, hands-on framing is what makes these roles winnable); and breadth-over-depth stack listing — three deep anchors beat a technology inventory.
