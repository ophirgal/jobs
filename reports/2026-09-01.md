## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Position as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience** — in that order. The evidence that carries the most weight in this specific set of openings is, ranked:

1. **Kubernetes-controller development** for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day. This is the differentiator. Most applicants at this level have *deployed onto* Kubernetes; building against the API is a different and rarer claim, and it is the mandatory line in #8 and the preferred line in #1, #7, #11 and #13.
2. **Go and Python as production languages**, not as familiarity. This is what makes #1, #4, #7, #8 and #12 reachable at all.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** In four of this week's roles (#4, #5, #6, #15) this moved from nice-to-have to mandatory. Say "evaluation" explicitly — it is the word the postings use.
4. **Throughput numbers.** 100k+ requests/day and 100k+ documents/day are the concrete production-scale proof; use them in the first two lines, not the third paragraph.
5. **MSc in Computer Science, UMD.** Cite it for Google (explicit preferred qualification) and NEXT Insurance (hard B.Sc. requirement), where it clears a stated bar.

**De-emphasize:** the freelance teaching years as engineering tenure — surface them only as evidence of mentoring and technical communication, never in the experience total; frontend/CSS depth (it invites the wrong role); academic ML and data-science research as a research identity (frame it as why AI-system evaluation is second nature, not as a research career); and anything that reads as DevOps, SRE or operations ownership — three of this week's near-misses were rejected precisely for being ops-heavy, and the candidate should not be sorted into that pile.
