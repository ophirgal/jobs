## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience** — not a generic full-stack developer. The single most differentiating line in this profile is *Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day*: it is infrastructure-grade backend work, it is quantified, and almost no engineer at the 2–3 year mark can claim it. Pair it with the 100k+ documents/day processing at Qualtrics so the throughput story reads as a pattern rather than one project.

Sequence the narrative as: **(1)** Go/Python distributed backend on Kubernetes and AWS → **(2)** generative-AI evaluation and AI-enabled product work → **(3)** TypeScript/React full-stack ownership → **(4)** MSc in CS, 3.9 GPA, and the teaching record as evidence of communication and mentoring.

**De-emphasise:** the freelance teaching years as though they were engineering tenure — cite them as communication and mentoring evidence only, and keep the professional-experience figure at 2–3 years so it is never read as inflated. Also de-emphasise pure ML/data-science research framing (it invites research-scientist screens the candidate does not want), generic "full-stack developer" phrasing, and HTML/CSS-level frontend detail. Where a posting names a language the candidate lacks (C#, Kotlin, Java, Node.js), address it in one line as transferable and move immediately back to the Go/Python/Kubernetes evidence.

**Israel — searched comprehensively.** Indeed connector sweeps across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ra'anana, Petah Tikva and Israel-wide, on backend/Go, backend/Python+Kubernetes, full-stack/React+Python, distributed systems, and AI-platform queries. Specific things that blocked or failed this run:

- **Built In's Israel region filter is not honoured server-side.** `builtin.com/jobs/mena/israel/dev-engineering` and its `/search/` sub-paths returned US listings on fetch — the region filter is applied client-side. Individual `builtin.com/job/<slug>/<id>` pages fetch correctly, so Israeli roles were verified one posting at a time rather than enumerated from a board. This is the largest single coverage limitation in the run and likely means Israeli Built In roles were missed.
- **Riskified careers page returned HTTP 403.** Not verified.
- **Greenhouse board slugs that returned 404:** Cyera, Coralogix, Island, Wiz. These companies host their boards elsewhere; not reached this run.
- **Axonius** board fetched successfully but listed only a Principal Full Stack Engineer in Israel (too senior) — no mid-level engineering openings.
- **LinkedIn Jobs and Glassdoor** were surfaced via WebSearch but not fetchable, as expected. No role was included on the strength of a search snippet alone.
- **Not reached this run for budget reasons:** Wellfound, Startup Nation Central, Israeli VC portfolio boards, and the Comeet-hosted boards generally (the one Comeet URL attempted resolved to Spark Hire's marketing site rather than a job board).
- **Indeed's Israel index skews stale.** A large share of returned Israeli postings dated to February–July 2026 and were excluded on the ~45-day freshness rule. Dice and ZipRecruiter were deliberately not used for Israel, per the operating brief.
