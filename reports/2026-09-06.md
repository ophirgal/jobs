## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes and AWS, with production experience at scale and hands-on generative-AI systems work.** Every top-ranked role in this run rewards that exact framing.

The three concrete proof points that should appear in the first four lines of any CV or outreach message:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day** — this is the differentiator; almost no 2–3-year engineer can claim controller-level Kubernetes work.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** — evidence of real scale, not toy projects.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — the requirement that converts Aikido, Skai, Lemonade, Onshore and Garner from "qualified" to "unusually well-suited."

Add **full-stack ownership (TypeScript/React over a Python or Go backend)** as a secondary line for Garner, Jeen.ai, CHEQ, ScyllaDB and the Google Chronicle req. Keep the **MSc Computer Science, University of Maryland (3.9)** prominent — it clears a hard gate at CHEQ (GPA > 90), Skai (B.Sc. CS or equivalent) and Google (advanced degree halves the experience minimum).

**De-emphasise:** freelance and vocational teaching years — list them as teaching, never blended into the engineering total, since several of these reqs sit at a 2–3 year bar the candidate cleanly meets and an inflated total invites a senior-level screen that would be failed. Also de-emphasise generic "full-stack developer" framing, pure frontend/CSS work, data-science and ML-research framing (it draws research screens), and any DevOps-first positioning — these roles want a software engineer who operates infrastructure, not an operator.
