## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with proven full-stack ownership and generative-AI product experience.** In practice the headline should be three concrete facts in the first two lines of every application: Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day; production systems processing 100k+ documents/day; and hands-on evaluation of generative-AI systems. That combination — infrastructure-grade Kubernetes work *plus* AI-system evaluation *plus* a React/TypeScript half — is what separates this profile from the large pool of 2–3 year backend applicants, and it is exactly what the AI-flavoured reqs (Aikido, Skai, Voyantis, Lemonade, Onshore, Garner) are screening for.

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years — they are a genuine strength for communication and mentoring, but listing them near the top invites a recruiter to read the professional timeline as shorter or less focused than it is; keep them as a single line under a separate heading. Also de-emphasise academic ML and data-science research when applying to product-engineering reqs — it reads as a signal toward research roles the candidate is not targeting. Do not present as Senior: nine of the fifteen roles here are banded at or below three years, and reaching for a senior title forfeits the strongest advantage, which is being over-qualified for the band rather than under-qualified.
