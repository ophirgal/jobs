## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed cloud services, with production full-stack ownership and hands-on generative-AI system evaluation.**

Concretely, the top of the résumé and every cover note should carry three numbers and one artefact: **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway**, **100k+ requests/day**, **100k+ documents/day**, and the **MSc in Computer Science from Maryland**. Those four items are what separate this candidate from the median applicant to a 2-year req — and, critically, from the median applicant to a *1-year* req like Google SecOps or Affirm, where the profile is genuinely over the bar rather than at it.

For the AI-adjacent reqs (#9 Aikido, #13 Skai, #11 ScyllaDB, #2 Affirm), position the generative-AI work as **evaluation and production-systems engineering** — building the evals, tracing the runs, proving the change helped — not as model research. That framing is what those postings actually ask for and it is where the record is strongest.

**De-emphasise:** the vocational-training and teaching-assistant history as *experience* (it is communication and mentoring evidence, and belongs in one line, not a block — it must never be allowed to read as a substitute for full-time engineering years); academic machine-learning research, which invites a data-science conversation the candidate does not want; and any framing that reads as generic "full-stack developer," which prices this profile alongside bootcamp-trained React engineers and discards the platform depth that is the actual differentiator.

**New York City — searched seriously, one new role cleared.** Built In NYC (entry/mid filter, 7-day window), Built In national with an NYC keyword pass, Dice NYC (113 postings in the last 7 days, faceted), and Indeed NYC. Four NYC-anchored roles are in the table. The single new one (Affirm) cleared on a 1.5-year floor plus a four-way stack match, not on company prestige.

**Washington, DC — searched seriously, zero roles included, and here is exactly why.** Dice DC (166 postings in the last 7 days) and Indeed DC/Northern Virginia were both swept. The DC metro's mid-level backend market is overwhelmingly federal-contractor work, and the postings split into two disqualifying groups. The larger group — Base-2 Solutions, North Point Technology, JTEC, NT Concepts, Markon, Benz Technology, Shawky Engineering, Leidos, VTG — requires an **active TS/SCI clearance, frequently with polygraph**. The candidate's U.S. citizenship is established by the Candidate Reference, but *holding or having held a clearance is not*, and these reqs need an existing adjudicated clearance rather than the eligibility to pursue one; per the report's own rules that is an eligibility gate that cannot be established, so those roles are excluded rather than listed with an unknown-risk flag. The smaller group (Vantor, SteerBridge, Two Six, Lumen Solutions, Capital One McLean) is senior, frontend-weighted, or data-engineering rather than software engineering. **No DC role reached the strict inclusion gate, and none was close enough to warrant an exception.** This is the same structural result as prior runs and is unlikely to change without a commercial-tech DC employer entering the mid-level market.
