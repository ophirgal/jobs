## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with a single sentence, everywhere: **Backend engineer who writes Kubernetes controllers in Go and Python for globally distributed, production-scale services — with full-stack TypeScript/React ownership and hands-on generative-AI evaluation experience.**

Concretely, front-load these four proof points in the CV summary and the first paragraph of every application:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the differentiator. It converts "knows Kubernetes" into "has built control-plane software," which almost no 2–3-year applicant can claim. It is the single strongest line for #1, #4, #11, #12 and #13.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** — the scale evidence for every data-and-backend req (#2, #11, #12).
3. **Generative-AI system improvement and evaluation** — not "used an LLM API," but *defining whether a change improved quality, latency or cost*. This is the framing that lands at #1, #14, and the AI reqs in the re-verified list.
4. **MSc + BSc in CS, University of Maryland, 3.9** — an explicit preferred qualification at Google (#1, #4), an explicit reward at Lendbuzz (#2), and a hard gate-clear elsewhere. For U.S. reqs it also anchors the U.S. education and work history.

**On U.S. applications, state citizenship in the first line.** A U.S.-citizen applicant applying from Israel is routinely misfiled as needing sponsorship; one sentence removes the most common silent rejection in the NYC pipeline.

**What to de-emphasise:**

* **Do not lead with "Full-Stack Developer."** It flattens the profile into the most crowded category in the Israeli market and buries the Kubernetes and Go evidence. Full-stack belongs as the *second* sentence — "with full-stack ownership" — not the headline. The exception is #7, #10 and #15, where the req is genuinely full-stack and backend-weighted.
* **De-emphasise the freelance teaching years as engineering tenure.** Reframe them as mentoring, technical communication and the ability to explain complex systems — genuine strengths for #10, #12 and #13, all of which demand direct stakeholder work — but never present them as adding to the 2–3 years of professional software engineering. A recruiter who catches the inflation discounts everything else on the page.
* **De-emphasise academic ML and data-science research** except where a req names AI evaluation or ML infrastructure. On a backend req it invites the wrong screen — data scientist rather than engineer.
* **Do not chase Senior, Staff, Lead or Architect titles.** Several strong Israeli reqs this run (CrowdStrike at 6+ years, Fundamental and Blockaid and PVML at 5+, Loora at 7+) were excluded for exactly this reason. Applying under those bars burns the candidate's name at companies worth approaching correctly in two years.
