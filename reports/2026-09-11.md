## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Position as: **"Backend / full-stack engineer specializing in Go and Python services on Kubernetes, with production AI-product experience."**

The résumé's headline should be three concrete artifacts, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — this is the differentiator and it is what is winning interviews in this market.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — the scale proof point.
3. **Generative-AI system improvement and evaluation** — the current-market multiplier; roughly a third of the roles verified today name LLM/agent work explicitly (NICE, Dataminr, ScyllaDB, Jeen.ai, Taboola, AppDome).

Add a fourth line only for the full-stack reqs (Jeen.ai, Novella, Narmi, Taboola, NICE): **React/TypeScript production ownership at Qualtrics**.

**De-emphasize:** freelance teaching years as though they were engineering tenure — list them as teaching/mentoring, never blended into the professional total; academic ML/data-science research as a headline (it reads as a research-scientist signal and works against mid-level product-engineering reqs — keep it as one line supporting the AI work); and any framing that implies senior/lead scope, which triggers a level mismatch on 2+ year reqs and loses the "comfortably above the bar" advantage that makes reqs like NEXT Insurance and Yext high-probability.
