1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day** — this is the strongest single line in the profile and it is doing most of the work in ranks 2, 4, 6, 12 and 13. Never bury it below the fold.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day**, with the text-analytics work named explicitly (classification, sentiment, summarisation) — this is what separates the candidate from the field at Dataminr, Jeen.ai and ScyllaDB.
3. **MSc + BSc Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** — matters most for the U.S. roles and for the NVIDIA/NICE multinational screens.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance teaching years.** Keep the teaching on the CV — it is real evidence of communication and mentoring, and rank 5 and rank 9 both reward that — but never let it sit where a screener might read it as a substitute for full-time engineering tenure. The professional total is ~2–3 years and should be stated plainly.
- **Generic "full-stack developer" framing.** It flattens the Go/Kubernetes/distributed-systems record into something indistinguishable from a React contractor.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** Cite it only where AI is already the subject (Dataminr, Jeen.ai, NICE, ScyllaDB). Elsewhere it invites the wrong level of role.
- **Breadth-for-its-own-sake technology lists.** Depth in Go, Python and Kubernetes outperforms a long tail of logos at this band.
