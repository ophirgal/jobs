## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services — with production full-stack ownership and real generative-AI evaluation experience.*

Three anchors, in this order, every time:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day.** The strongest single line in the profile. It is doing the primary work in ranks 1, 4, 6, 7 and 14. Never bury it.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day**, with the text-analytics work named explicitly (classification, sentiment, summarisation). This is what separates the candidate from the field at Dataminr, Aikido, Jeen.ai and DeepL.
3. **MSc + BSc Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA.** This run gave the MSc a concrete, mechanical value it did not have before: at Google it **halves the stated experience minimum**. Put it above the fold on any application to a company that publishes an advanced-degree equivalency, and mention it in every U.S. application.

**A note on framing for the AI roles.** Aikido, DeepL, Dataminr, Jeen.ai and NICE are five of the fifteen. For all five, the correct frame is **"I build and evaluate AI systems in production"** — never "I'm interested in AI" and never "I did ML research." The evaluation and measurement work is the scarce credential; the research background is not.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance teaching years.** Keep them on the CV — they are real evidence of communication and mentoring, and ranks 8 and 12 reward that — but never where a screener could read them as substituting for full-time engineering tenure. State the professional total plainly as ~2–3 years.
- **Generic "full-stack developer" framing.** It flattens the Go/Kubernetes/distributed-systems record into something indistinguishable from a React contractor.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** Cite it only where AI is already the subject, and even there, subordinate it to the production-evaluation work.
- **Breadth-for-its-own-sake technology lists.** Depth in Go, Python and Kubernetes outperforms a long tail of logos at this band.
