## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services — with production full-stack ownership and real generative-AI evaluation experience.*

Three anchors, in this order, every time:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day.** The strongest single line in the profile. It is doing the primary work in ranks 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and 15. Never bury it.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day**, with the text-analytics work named explicitly (classification, sentiment, summarisation). This is what separates the candidate from the field at Dataminr, Aikido, Jeen.ai, DeepL and Speechify.
3. **MSc + BSc Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA.** At Google it **mechanically halves the stated experience minimum**. Put it above the fold on any application to a company publishing an advanced-degree equivalency, and mention it in every U.S. application.

**A note on the two new roles.** Speechify and Bringg are both TypeScript/Node-leaning shops, and they are the first entries in several runs where the candidate's *strongest* technologies are not the ones being asked for. For those two applications specifically, invert the usual emphasis: lead with **distributed systems, PostgreSQL, event-driven services and production debugging** — the language-neutral engineering substance — and present Go and Python as evidence of depth rather than as the headline. Do not do this anywhere else in the report; for the other thirteen roles the Go/Python/Kubernetes headline *is* the advantage.

**A note on framing for the AI roles.** Aikido, DeepL, Dataminr, Jeen.ai, NICE and Speechify are six of the fifteen. For all six the correct frame is **"I build and evaluate AI systems in production"** — never "I'm interested in AI" and never "I did ML research." The evaluation and measurement work is the scarce credential; the research background is not.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance teaching years.** Keep them on the CV — they are real evidence of communication and mentoring, and ranks 8 and 12 reward that — but never where a screener could read them as substituting for full-time engineering tenure. State the professional total plainly as ~2–3 years.
- **Generic "full-stack developer" framing.** It flattens the Go/Kubernetes/distributed-systems record into something indistinguishable from a React contractor.
