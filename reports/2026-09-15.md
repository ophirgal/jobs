## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.** Given that this run surfaced three new AI-axis roles and that the two strongest new openings (monday.com render.ai, WorldQuant) are AI-product engineering reqs, the AI-systems line should move from the second paragraph of the CV to the first.

The three facts that should appear in the first six lines of every application:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the single most transferable asset in the profile and it maps onto platform, infrastructure, backend *and* API roles.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** — the scale claim that makes "high-throughput" credible rather than aspirational.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — evals, scorers, datasets, offline testing. Almost nobody at the 2–3 year band has this, and four roles in this table ask for it by name.

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years (mention as evidence of mentoring and communication, never as engineering tenure — and never in a way that muddies the 2–3 year figure); pure frontend/CSS work; academic ML research framed as research rather than as applied engineering judgement; and any framing that reads as Senior, Lead or Architect. The banding advantage in this report is real — nine of fifteen roles have a bar of 2 years or less — and over-claiming seniority forfeits it.
