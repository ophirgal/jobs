## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend / Platform Engineer in Go and Python who builds Kubernetes-native, cloud-scale services — with production generative-AI experience as a differentiator.** Concretely, open with the **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day**: it is the single most credible artefact in the profile, it is simultaneously backend, distributed-systems and platform work, and it is what reqs #1, #2, #3 and #10 are all actually asking for.

Carry three supporting threads: **production scale** (100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics), **AI-system evaluation** (not prompt-writing — measured evaluation, which is what monday.com, Aikido and WorldQuant are short of), and the **MSc in CS**, which for both Google reqs is not a credential but a literal halving of the required years.

**De-emphasise:** the freelance teaching years as *engineering* experience — present them under communication and mentoring, never in the experience total, since conflating them invites a credibility problem exactly where the candidate is already at the band's edge. Also de-emphasise frontend depth (React/CSS implementation detail) outside the explicitly full-stack roles, the academic ML research where it competes with the production-engineering story, and any framing as "generalist full-stack developer," which discards the specificity that makes this profile competitive.

## 🔹 Search Gaps

**Israel — searched comprehensively.** Built In Israel (backend, full-stack, software-engineer and mid-level filtered listings), the Indeed connector across Tel Aviv, Ramat Gan, Herzliya and Kfar Saba, plus targeted re-verification of all 15 previously reported roles. Coverage this run was good and produced four new roles. Specific gaps: **LinkedIn Jobs Israel** blocks automated fetch as always and was reachable only indirectly through search-engine indexing, which is not verification and so contributed nothing to the table; **Startup Nation Central** and **Israeli VC portfolio boards** (Aleph, TLV Partners, Team8) were not reached this run — Team8 surfaced only incidentally via an Indeed listing. Those boards are the most likely place a genuinely hidden Israeli req is sitting, and they are the first thing to spend budget on next run.

**New York City — searched actively, one new candidate considered and rejected.** Covered via Built In NYC (mid-level backend filters) and two Indeed connector passes (Go/Kubernetes backend; Kubernetes/Go platform). The market's structural problem was visible again: nearly everything returned was **Senior, Staff or Lead** — Gen Digital, Hex, Thomson Reuters, Collectors, Hinge, Merciv, Capital One — none of which the candidate should apply to. The one serious new candidate was **Nominal (Software Engineer – Backend, New York, $130–230K, 3+ years, Go/Rust/Java with Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform and AWS)**. It was **excluded** on two grounds. First, the posting makes storage-and-streaming specialisation a core requirement — *"Hands-on experience designing or operating storage systems: time-series databases, columnar formats, or analytics engines (ClickHouse, TimescaleDB, Druid, InfluxDB, Parquet)"* and *"streaming or data processing technologies such as Kafka, Apache Arrow, Flink, or Spark"* — and neither is evidenced anywhere in the profile. Under the U.S. gate that is a **major specialised-domain requirement the candidate lacks**, which makes it Medium at best and therefore excludable by rule. Second, it was posted **4 August, roughly 43 days ago**, right at the staleness limit. Worth noting for completeness: its **ITAR clause** (U.S. citizen, permanent resident, refugee or asylee) is *satisfied* — the candidate is a U.S. citizen — so eligibility was not the reason for exclusion. The three U.S. roles in the table (Garner Health, DeepL, Dataminr) are all previously reported and all re-verified live today.

**Washington, DC — searched actively, zero roles included.** One Indeed connector pass across the DC metro (DC, Arlington, McLean, Reston, Chantilly, Herndon, Germantown, Laurel) plus a targeted web search for DC backend roles on Greenhouse/Lever at the 2–3 year band. The result was almost entirely **cleared defense and intelligence contracting** — Leidos, Expedition Technology, Prescient Edge, Vantor, Insight Xcite, Rune Technologies, JHU APL — where postings carry security-clearance conditions the candidate's eligibility for cannot be established from available evidence, and which the exclusion rules treat as disqualifying rather than as an unknown to gamble on. What remained was either senior (Capital One Lead/Senior Lead in McLean), a graduate programme (JHU APL 2027), Java/Spring consulting (Solution Street), or too thin to verify (Foundation Stack AI, McLean). **Nothing cleared the gate, and nothing was included to demonstrate that DC was searched.** DC's structural issue for this profile is durable, not a one-run artefact: the metro's backend demand is concentrated in cleared work, and until clearance eligibility is established the addressable pool is small. A commercial-only DC pass (fintech, health-tech, SaaS around Bethesda and Northern Virginia) is the better use of budget next run than repeating a general metro sweep.

One flag worth raising: that résumé records **"Willing to Relocate: No"** and a minimum-salary preference denominated in **GBP** — both look like stale or mis-entered profile settings, and the relocation flag in particular may be suppressing the candidate from U.S. recruiter searches on Indeed. Worth correcting at profile.indeed.com.
