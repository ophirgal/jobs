## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput cloud services, with full-stack delivery and applied-AI experience.**

Concretely, open every application and conversation with the two quantified artifacts — **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day**, and **production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** — because they simultaneously answer "scale," "distributed systems," "Kubernetes" and "production ownership," which are the four requirements that recur across almost every posting in this table. Add the **MSc in Computer Science (3.9 GPA, University of Maryland)** early for Google specifically, where it is worth a full year of the stated experience minimum.

**De-emphasize:** freelance/vocational teaching as a *duration* claim — it is excellent evidence of communication and mentoring, but listing it near the professional timeline invites recruiters to misread total years; keep it in a clearly separate section. Also de-emphasize generic "full-stack developer" framing, academic ML/data-science research as a headline (it reads as a research-scientist signal these teams screen against), and breadth-of-frameworks lists — depth in Go/Python/Kubernetes outranks a long technology inventory for every role ranked above.
