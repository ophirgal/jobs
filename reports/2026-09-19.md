## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput cloud services, with production experience building and evaluating AI-enabled systems.** Three concrete proof points carry nearly every role in this table: the **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at over 100k requests/day** (SAP), the **pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics), and **generative-AI evaluation and improvement** (SAP). The MSc is not decoration — at both Google reqs it is the clause that halves the experience requirement, so state it in the first line of any Google application.

Add, secondarily, **full-stack ownership with TypeScript and React** — it is what makes DoiT, Linx's second req, Stigg and Garner reachable — and **independent internal-tooling delivery**, which is the specific hook for Mobileye and AppDome.

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years as engineering tenure (keep them as evidence of communication and mentoring, stated as such); breadth-first "generalist full-stack developer" framing, which reads as junior in the Israeli market; CSS, design-system and UI-implementation depth; academic ML and data-science research, which invites mis-routing toward research or data-science reqs this profile should not be in; and any hint of Staff/Lead/Architect ambition — the whole strategy here depends on landing mid-level bands where the candidate is *inside* the range rather than reaching for it.
