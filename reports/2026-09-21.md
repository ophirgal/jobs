Lead with: **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production full-stack range and hands-on generative-AI system experience.**

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the single most differentiating line in the profile. 2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** Throughput, correctness under load, and ownership — the hook for #4, #10, #11 and #15.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement, plus text analytics, classification, sentiment analysis and summarisation.** Increasingly the differentiator rather than a footnote: it is what makes #3, #12 and #15 credible rather than speculative, and it is the reason to prefer an AI-infrastructure company like Lightning AI over a generic SaaS backend.
4. **MSc + BSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA.** Not a garnish — it is a *mechanical* qualification change at Google (2 years → 1 year) and a stated hard requirement at Mobileye and Bond Sports.

**De-emphasize:**

- **Freelance teaching and vocational instruction.** It is genuine evidence of communication and mentoring, but listing it prominently invites a reader to net it against the ~2–3 years of full-time engineering, which is the opposite of what it should do. Fold it into a single line under communication/mentoring, never into the experience timeline.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** It reads as a signal toward research roles, which are explicitly out of scope, and it dilutes the production-engineering narrative. Keep it only where the posting is AI-adjacent (#3, #12, #15).
- **Frontend breadth for its own sake.** HTML/CSS and design-system fluency invite frontend-role routing. Present React and TypeScript strictly as *full-stack ownership over a backend you built* — which is how #9, #11 and #14 want to read it.
- **Any framing that reaches for Senior.** The strongest roles in this table (#3, #5, #7, #12, #13) are entry-level or junior-banded requisitions where the candidate applies *from above*. That is the advantage. Reaching for Senior forfeits it and moves the candidate into a pool where 2–3 years is a deficit rather than a match.
