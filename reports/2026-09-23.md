## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with full-stack product ownership and production generative-AI experience.*

Three concrete claims should appear in the first four lines of any CV or message, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day (SAP).** This is the rarest thing on the profile. Very few engineers with ~3 years have written controllers rather than merely deployed to a cluster, and it is the differentiator in roles 1, 4, 5, 6, 12 and 13.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day, with text analytics, classification, sentiment and summarization (Qualtrics).** This is what converts roles 3 and 10 from "qualified" to "obvious hire."
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation.** Scarce, current, and explicitly wanted at Genesys, Dataminr, Tailor Brands and Skai. Frame it as *evaluation and production integration*, never as research.

**De-emphasize:**
- **Freelance teaching as engineering tenure.** It is a genuine asset — communication, mentoring, technical explanation — and belongs in the mentorship line that Tailor Brands explicitly asks for. It is not professional software-engineering experience and should never be counted toward a year total; that inflation is the fastest way to lose credibility in a screen.
- **Academic ML/data-science research**, except where a posting names ML infrastructure (roles 6 and 10). Elsewhere it invites misclassification as a research candidate for hands-on engineering roles.
- **Breadth-first framing** ("full-stack generalist"). Nine of the fifteen roles here are backend, platform or infrastructure. Generic full-stack positioning discards the Kubernetes and Go edge, which is the whole argument.
- **Seniority ambiguity.** Apply as a strong mid-level engineer. The 0–3 year bands are where this profile wins outright; reaching for senior titles converts clear wins into rejections.

**Reached and verified:** the Indeed connector across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ra'anana, Ramat Gan, Petah Tikva, Kfar Saba, Hod Hasharon and Rishon LeZion; builtin.com Israel listings and individual postings; freehire.me aggregated Israel listings for backend, full-stack, AI-engineering and DevOps, including junior/mid seniority filters; and official ATS pages at Greenhouse (Garner, ERGO NEXT), Comeet, Breezy (Zoomd), plus the company-hosted career pages of Upwind, Tailor Brands and Oobit.

**Could not verify / did not reach:**
- **builtin.com's location filter is broken for Israel on the generic `/jobs/dev-engineering` and keyword-search routes** — it silently returns U.S. listings. Only the `/jobs/mena/israel/...` route filters correctly, and that route's result set is narrower than the site's true Israel inventory. Individual Israel job pages fetch correctly; the listing layer is the problem.
- **LinkedIn Jobs** — not fetchable, as in every prior run. Worked around via web search plus pivoting to company ATS pages, but LinkedIn-exclusive Israeli postings remain invisible to this pipeline. This is the largest single blind spot in the Israel search.
- **Glassdoor** — repeatedly surfaced in search results but is login-walled and was never treated as a source.
- **Startup Nation Central and Israeli VC portfolio boards** (Team8, Meron, Techstars) appeared only as indirect search hits; their own boards were not crawled directly this run.
- **devjobs.co.il** returned HTTP 500.
- **Dice and ZipRecruiter** were not spent on Israel, per the operating brief — they carry effectively no Israeli inventory.

**Structural observation, consistent across three consecutive runs:** the Israeli market is currently posting overwhelmingly at **senior and above**. freehire's own count today: of **184 open full-stack roles in Israel, 4 junior and 2 middle**; of **267 backend roles, 4 at junior or middle**. Roles verified and excluded today purely on an out-of-band experience requirement or wrong stack: HUMAN Security (4+ yrs), Cycode (4+ yrs, .NET), Fullpath (4–5 yrs **plus mandatory fluent Hebrew**), Claroty (5+ yrs), Coralogix (5+ yrs, Scala/Rust), G2 Risk Solutions (7+ yrs Java), Nexxen (5+ yrs), ERGO NEXT AI Engineer (5+ yrs), Guideline/Hashavim (5+ yrs), Popai Health via Team8 (5+ yrs), BioCatch (5+ yrs, frontend SDK), Medtronic/CathWorks (4+ yrs, C/C++ medical imaging), Apono (6+ yrs), NielsenIQ (4+ yrs, **and** Yokneam Illit — outside commute range — **and** 47 days old), Google Filestore (low-level C/C++ filesystem internals), Google Haifa Technical Infrastructure (outside commute range). **The shortage of new Israeli entries this run reflects the market, not the search.**

### 🇺🇸 Washington, DC — searched actively, zero roles cleared the gate
**Reached and verified:** the Indeed connector for Washington DC (returning DC proper plus Arlington, McLean, Tysons and Fort Meade), with full job-detail retrieval on the most promising result.

**Why nothing cleared:** the DC-metro engineering market surfaced this run is dominated by **cleared defence and intelligence contracting**. Results included ManTech (Fort Meade), Markon (Fort Meade, $215–225K), Prescient Edge, Data Surge (explicit **Secret Clearance** requirement) and Booz Allen — all either clearance-gated, stale (April/June postings), or senior. The single closest candidate, **Foundation Stack AI — Full Stack Developer (McLean, VA; $125–150K; posted 13 days ago)**, was retrieved in full and **deliberately excluded**. It requires U.S. citizenship and willingness to undergo security clearance, which the candidate — a U.S. citizen — could satisfy; but its mandatory requirements are **JavaScript, React and Next.js**, with Python, gRPC, Docker and Kubernetes listed only under "It's even better if you have." That is a frontend-weighted role where the candidate's backend, Go and Kubernetes strengths sit in the optional column, which makes it a **Medium at best — and Medium NYC/DC roles are excluded by the inclusion gate.** Prestige, mission and clearance-eligibility do not substitute for candidate fit.

### ⚠️ Standing eligibility note — U.S. location, not U.S. authorization
Work authorization is settled: the candidate is a U.S. citizen requiring no sponsorship, and this is a genuine advantage at Garner Health and Lightning AI, both of which explicitly decline to sponsor. **What is not established is U.S. physical presence.** The Indeed connector's `get_resume` tool was called at the start of this run; it returned a thin, partially garbled profile that corroborates the authoritative Candidate Reference on education (UMD MSc and BSc), skills (Go, Python, Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, SQL, React, JavaScript, REST APIs, microservices) and the teaching history — but it also carries a stored preference of **"Willing to Relocate: No."** That is a job-board preference toggle, not an authoritative statement, and it is not being treated as one. It is flagged because **every NYC role in this table requires physical relocation**, and because it is the reason Affirm's remote-anywhere-in-the-U.S. posting was held below the cut line rather than ranked. No relocation assumption has been made in either direction.

**Security clearance:** no clearance is claimed or assumed anywhere in this report. Where a DC role required one, it was excluded on fit grounds before clearance ever became the deciding factor.
