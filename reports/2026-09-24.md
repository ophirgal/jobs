## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Position as a **Backend / Platform Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for production-scale cloud services, with demonstrated generative-AI product and evaluation experience.** Two proof points should appear in the first three lines of every application: **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day (SAP)** and **production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day (Qualtrics)**. Scale numbers do the work that a fourth year of experience would otherwise do.

This run reinforces a specific opportunity: **the generative-AI clause is becoming a gating requirement rather than a bonus.** Google's Notification Platform requisition makes LLM-integration experience *mandatory*; Aikido's is built entirely around AI evaluation; Garner, Tailor Brands, ScyllaDB, Oobit and High Lander all name AI coding tools explicitly. The candidate's SAP generative-AI evaluation work is now a **primary differentiator, not a footnote** — it should be promoted to the second sentence of the CV summary.

Where full-stack roles are in play, frame as **backend-weighted full-stack** and let the React/TypeScript Qualtrics work be the supporting clause, never the headline.

**De-emphasize:** freelance and vocational teaching as though it were engineering tenure — keep it as one line evidencing mentoring and communication, which Tailor Brands and WalkMe-class postings explicitly ask for, and nothing more. Also de-emphasize academic ML research framing (it invites a research screen for roles that are production engineering), generic "full-stack developer" language, and any implication of architect or lead scope.

## 🔹 Search Gaps

**Israel — searched comprehensively.** Indeed connector queries were run across Tel Aviv, Ramat Gan, Herzliya, Ra'anana, Kfar Saba, Hod Hasharon, Petah Tikva, Rishon LeZion and Israel-wide, across backend/full-stack/platform/AI-engineer keyword families. Official career pages fetched live: Tailor Brands, Upwind, Oobit, Aikido, Komodor, Port (Comeet). Builtin job pages fetched live for nine individual requisitions.

- **Built In's Israel-filtered search pages did not hold their location filter under automated fetch this run.** `builtin.com/jobs/mena/israel/dev-engineering/search/backend-engineer` and the `full-stack-engineer` equivalent both returned **U.S. listings only**. The generic Israel software-engineer page did work and surfaced Taboola, Speechify (Haifa and Jerusalem) and both Mobileye ML requisitions. Individual builtin job URLs fetched normally throughout. This is a real coverage hole in the Israeli long tail — it is the source that has historically produced the most mid-level Israeli finds.
- **Speechify — Software Engineer, Platform** appeared live in Haifa (2 days old) and Jerusalem (7 days). Both were excluded on **location**: Haifa and Jerusalem sit outside the stated central-Israel commute envelope. Flagging them here in case that constraint ever loosens.
- **LinkedIn Jobs, Glassdoor and Israeli-local boards** remain unfetchable under automation. Search-engine snippets referencing them were used only as leads and were always chased to a retrievable source before anything entered the report. A Via backend role surfaced this way and was excluded at **6+ years**.
- **Not reached this run:** Wellfound, Startup Nation Central, and Israeli VC portfolio boards were not worked directly — budget went to verifying the 22 carryover roles first. These are the highest-value targets for the next run.

**New York City — searched, one role sustained, no new additions.** Indeed (Go/Kubernetes backend, Python/React SE II) and ZipRecruiter (mid/junior filters, Go + Python + Kubernetes skills, 30-day window) were both run. Results were dominated by senior/staff bands, staffing agencies and remote-anywhere roles. Two were assessed seriously and **deliberately excluded**:

- **Nominal — Software Engineer, Backend** (NYC, 3+ years, $130–230K, ITAR-compliant so citizenship is satisfiable). Go, Kubernetes, Docker and AWS are all direct hits. Excluded because the requirements list **hands-on experience designing or operating time-series/columnar storage systems (ClickHouse, TimescaleDB, Druid, InfluxDB, Parquet) and streaming stacks (Kafka, Arrow, Flink, Spark)** as core bullets the candidate does not hold — that is a genuine mandatory gap, which the strict U.S. gate does not forgive. It would be a defensible **Medium**, and Medium does not belong in this report.
- **BlackRock — Associate, Software Engineer (Aladdin Graph)**, YO AI Labs, Premier Global Links and Raydar were reviewed from the ZipRecruiter result set and not pursued: respectively a Java-weighted platform, a remote-anywhere listing, a staffing intermediary, and a senior band.

**Washington, DC — searched seriously, zero roles, and the reason is structural.** Indeed (Python/Kubernetes SWE, DC) and ZipRecruiter (mid/junior, Python + Kubernetes + Go, DC, 30-day window) both returned essentially the same population: **Booz Allen, ManTech, Leidos, HII, Northstrat, Benz Technology, Tensley, Halogen** — defense and intelligence contractors in Arlington, Chantilly, Fort Meade, Annapolis Junction and Andrews AFB. The recurring blockers were (a) **active TS/SCI-with-polygraph clearance required at time of application**, which available evidence cannot establish the candidate holds, (b) **DevOps/cloud-operations scope** rather than hands-on software engineering, and (c) **senior, lead or principal bands**. One non-cleared option, **Foundation Stack AI — Full Stack Developer** (McLean, VA; $125–150K; hybrid one day/week), was pulled and read in full: U.S. citizenship is satisfiable and clearance is only prospective, but its **minimum** requirements are JavaScript, React and Next.js, with Python, Docker and Kubernetes relegated to "even better if" — a **frontend-weighted** role that both the exclusion list and the strict DC gate rule out. Zero DC roles is the honest result, not a search failure.
