## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with a single sentence and keep it consistent across every application:

> **Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes, with production experience operating globally distributed API infrastructure and evaluating generative-AI systems.**

The three proof points that should appear above the fold on every CV sent this week:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day** — this is the strongest single line in the profile and it maps onto Linx, Upwind, Rise, Lightning AI, DeepL and all four Google requisitions.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** — scale evidence that is concrete and verifiable, and near-literal for Dataminr.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation** — now a *mandatory or named* qualification at Google Notification Platform, Aikido, Genesys and Dataminr, not a differentiator-in-waiting. Say what was measured and what improved.

Keep **TypeScript and React** as a clearly labelled secondary capability. It unlocks DoiT, Chronicle Full Stack, Dataminr and DeepL's preferred list without ever becoming the headline.

**De-emphasise:** the vocational-training and teaching-assistant history as *experience* — it belongs under a short "Teaching & Mentoring" heading, never merged into the professional-engineering timeline, because conflating them invites a seniority mismatch. Also de-emphasise pure frontend and CSS work, academic ML research framed as research rather than engineering, and any breadth-over-depth "familiar with N technologies" list. The profile is strongest when it reads as *narrow and deep on backend infrastructure*, not broad and general.
