## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with a single sentence, everywhere: **"Backend engineer working in Go and Python on Kubernetes and AWS, with production experience on high-scale distributed services and on evaluating and improving generative-AI systems."**

Concretely, front-load these four artefacts in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day** — this is the single most differentiating item and it should appear in the first two lines of every application.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** — the credential for scale, and the bridge into any retrieval, search, NLP, or document-AI role (Yext, Draftwise, Dataminr).
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement** — now a *mandatory* requirement at Workday, Google's Notification Platform, Aikido, and Centrical, not a bonus. Describe it in the language of evaluation design and measured improvement, not experimentation.
4. **MSc CS, University of Maryland, 3.9** — clears "advanced degree" shortcuts at Google (1 year with an advanced degree instead of 2) and is a live networking asset in DC.

**De-emphasize:** the freelance and vocational teaching years — mention them once as evidence of communication and mentoring, never as engineering tenure, and never in a way that inflates the years-of-experience count; pure-frontend framing of the Qualtrics work; academic ML and data-science research, which pulls toward research-scientist reqs the profile is not aimed at; and any implication of Staff, Lead, or architect scope. The strongest version of this candidate is an unambiguous, technically deep **mid-level engineer**, not an understated senior one.
