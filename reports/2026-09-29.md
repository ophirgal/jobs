## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with a single sentence and hold it across every application:

> **Backend engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes on cloud-native production systems, with hands-on generative-AI platform experience — currently at SAP, previously full-stack at Qualtrics, MSc in Computer Science.**

Three concrete proof points, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day.** This is the strongest single line in the profile. It proves Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems and production ownership simultaneously, and it is what makes ScyllaDB, Rise, Lightning AI, Vercel and ControlMonkey plausible at once.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day**, with text classification, sentiment and summarization. This carries the Yext retrieval role, the Qualtrics-to-Garner data-pipeline story, and anything AI-adjacent.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** In 2026 this is a differentiator, not a footnote. Workday, Google, Citizen, Draftwise, Voyantis and Centrical all name LLM work in their mandatory requirements — say it in the first paragraph, not the last.

**What to de-emphasize:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching.** Convert it into "mentoring and technical communication" inside the engineering narrative; never let it read as though it counts toward the professional engineering total. Postings tagged entry level and 1–2 years are the target band, and blurring the count invites a senior-level screen the candidate will lose.
