## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend / Platform Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production full-stack range and hands-on generative-AI system experience.** Concretely, the four assets to put first, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day** — the single most differentiating line, and the one that unlocks Rise, Lightning AI, Voyantis, Linx, ScyllaDB and ControlMonkey.
2. **Go and Python as first-class production languages**, not exposure — named as a hard requirement at Chronicle, Yext, Garner, DoiT, Lightning AI and Vercel.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — a *minimum* qualification at Google Notification Platform, the core of Workday Paradox and Voyantis, and increasingly a tiebreaker everywhere else. Frame it as **evaluation and integration judgment**, not model training.
4. **MSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** — this is not decoration. At both Google reqs it is the mechanism that halves the experience minimum, and at ControlMonkey it is what backs the language-neutral "fundamentals" claim.

**What to de-emphasize:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Real evidence of communication and mentoring; not evidence of professional software-engineering tenure. Keep it as one line under a "Teaching / Mentoring" heading, never merged into the engineering timeline — several of these reqs sit at a 2–3 year bar where an inflated total invites the wrong screen.
* **Frontend breadth for its own sake.** React and TypeScript are an asset *because* they make the candidate a backend engineer who can own a feature end to end. Presented as general full-stack ability, the profile reads as a generic full-stack developer and loses to specialists at every backend req here.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** Useful context for the AI roles; actively misleading for backend reqs, where it invites a research-scientist read the candidate does not want.
* **Breadth of tools over depth of systems.** These postings reward production-scale ownership and debugging, not a long technology list.
