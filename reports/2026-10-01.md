## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for production cloud systems, with full-stack range and AI-product experience.*

The ordered proof points, strongest first:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at SAP, serving 100k+ requests/day.** This is the differentiator and it should open every application. It is simultaneously backend engineering, distributed-systems work, platform engineering and Go — four of the five things these postings ask for. Ranks 2, 4, 11, 12 and 13 are all winnable primarily on this one line.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** Concrete throughput, and the bridge into every data-platform, streaming or pipeline req (ranks 6, 7).
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement.** No longer a nice-to-have — it is a *stated minimum qualification* at Google (rank 1) and a *stated requirement* at High Lander (rank 4). Lead with it where postings name it.
4. **Full-stack TypeScript/React with backend ownership.** Deploy selectively, for ranks 3, 10 and the Stigg role — not in backend-only applications, where it dilutes the specialist framing.
5. **MSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland.** Mechanically valuable, not decorative: it reduces the stated experience floor at both Google reqs (ranks 2, 3) and is a listed preferred qualification at three postings in this report.

**De-emphasize:**
- **Freelance teaching years.** Mention the mentoring and communication strength, never the duration — a reader who sees teaching years adjacent to engineering years may read the total as inflated and the engineering as thinner than it is.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** It invites misrouting toward data-science and research-scientist reqs, which are explicitly off-target. Convert it into "evaluating and improving production AI systems" instead.
