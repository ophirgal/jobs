## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed services, with production generative-AI experience.**

The three evidence anchors, in priority order:

1. **Kubernetes-controller development for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the single highest-value line in the profile. It is what makes ranks 2, 3, 12, 13 and 14 credible, and it is platform-engineering evidence, not "I have used Kubernetes".
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** Throughput at a named number, which is what Affirm's streaming req (rank 8) and Taboola's distributed-systems req actually want to hear.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** No longer a differentiator but a scored requirement — ScyllaDB (rank 11), Workday (rank 15), Voyantis and Garner all name AI tooling or LLM products in the requisition text.

Add the **MSc in Computer Science** early for every Google application specifically: it is not a soft credential there, it mechanically halves the stated experience floor from two years to one.

**De-emphasise:** the vocational-instructor and teaching-assistant years as experience — they are communication and mentoring evidence and should be framed only that way, never counted toward the professional-engineering total; frontend-only framing (React and TypeScript are supporting evidence of full-stack ownership, not the headline); academic ML and data-science research, which pulls toward research-scientist screening that this candidate should not be in; and any claim of Kafka, Flink, Scala or Rust depth, where honesty is cheaper than a failed technical screen.
