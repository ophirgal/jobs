## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput distributed services, with production generative-AI experience.**

The three evidence anchors, in priority order:

1. **Kubernetes-controller development for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** The single highest-value line in the profile. It is what makes ranks 1, 3, 11, 12, 14 and 15 credible, and it is platform-engineering evidence, not "I have used Kubernetes."
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** Throughput at a named number — which is what Affirm's streaming req and Taboola's 950k-requests/second platform actually want to hear, and what substitutes for a missing Kafka/Flink line.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** This run makes the shift unambiguous: it is no longer a differentiator but a *stated requirement*. **Samsara** asks for AI evaluation frameworks, **Lendbuzz** for critical review of AI-generated code, **PAPAYA** for hands-on AI coding agents in production, and Linx, ScyllaDB, High Lander, Workday, Voyantis and Garner all name AI tooling in the requisition. Put it in the first third of the CV, not the last.

Add the **MSc in Computer Science** early for every Google application specifically: it is not a soft credential there, it mechanically halves the stated experience floor from two years to one.

**De-emphasise:** the vocational-instructor and teaching-assistant years as experience — they are communication and mentoring evidence and should be framed only that way, never counted toward the professional-engineering total (Linx rank 1 is the one req where the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences is a scored requirement, so frame it there and nowhere else); frontend-only framing, since React and TypeScript are supporting evidence of full-stack ownership rather than the headline; academic ML and data-science research, which pulls toward research-scientist screening this candidate should not be in; and any claim of Kafka, Flink, Scala, Rust or Next.js depth, where honesty is cheaper than a failed technical screen.
