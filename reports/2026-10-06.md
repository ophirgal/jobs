## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.**

The three-sentence version to open every application with:

> Software engineer with ~2–3 years building production backend systems in **Go and Python**. At SAP I develop **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100,000+ requests per day**; at Qualtrics I built production applications processing **100,000+ documents per day**. I hold an **MSc in Computer Science** and have hands-on experience improving and evaluating **generative-AI systems**.

Why this ordering works for *this* specific set of openings: the Kubernetes-controller line is the single most distinctive asset in the profile and it is named as a requirement by nine of the fifteen ranked roles (Appdome, Linx ×2, ScyllaDB, Fluidstack, CoreWeave, Raydar, GitLab, plus Upwind below the cut). The generative-AI line is what converts the two Google reqs and Workday from "qualified" to "differentiated" — Google's Notification Platform req makes it a *minimum*. And the throughput numbers (100k+ requests/day, 100k+ documents/day) do the work that years-of-experience cannot: they let a two-to-three-year candidate credibly answer a "production scale" requirement.

**Emphasise:** Kubernetes-controller development · globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day · Go and Python backend ownership · 100k+ documents/day pipelines · Docker, AWS, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, distributed services · generative-AI evaluation and LLM integration · independently built internal engineering tools · root-cause analysis and production debugging · MSc Computer Science, UMD (3.9).

**De-emphasise or reframe:**
* **Do not lead with "full-stack."** React and TypeScript are real assets, but framed first they read as generic full-stack and pull the candidacy toward frontend roles that are explicitly out of scope. Use full-stack as a *secondary* qualifier — "full-stack with backend ownership" — and only for the Linx Full Stack, ScyllaDB, DoiT and CoreWeave applications.
* **Do not present teaching years as engineering years.** The teaching and head-TA record is strong evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — and should be used that way, especially for GitLab (async written communication) and the Google reqs — but the professional-engineering figure must stay at ~2–3 years. Conflating them invites a credibility problem at exactly the moment banding is being assessed.
* **Do not foreground academic ML/data-science research.** It risks routing the candidate into pure data-science or research pipelines, which are out of scope. Convert it into *applied* signal: AI-system evaluation, text analytics, classification, summarisation.
* **Do not volunteer the Java/C#/Ruby gaps.** Several strong roles (Google Filestore, Yext, Taboola, GitLab, ControlMonkey) name a language the candidate lacks. Lead with transferable systems depth and CS fundamentals; address the language only when asked, and then with a concrete ramp story.
* **Avoid Senior/Staff/Lead framing entirely.** Every role in this table is mid-level or entry-banded scope, and this run's rejections were overwhelmingly caused by seniority mismatch in the *other* direction. There is nothing to gain from inflating.
