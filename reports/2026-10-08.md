## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes, building and operating distributed cloud services at production scale — with hands-on generative-AI system evaluation and full-stack TypeScript/React capability behind it.*

Four concrete claims should appear in the first third of the CV, because this run's requirement texts reward all four directly:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day.** This is the single highest-leverage line available. It is mandatory or desired at ranks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 13 and 15. Crucially, the word *globally distributed* is what answers the **multi-region** and **distributed systems** asks at Stash, Salesforce and Google — a 3-year engineer with single-region experience cannot make that claim.
2. **Go and Python as co-primary backend languages.** Ranks 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 13 and 15 name one or both as accepted primary languages. Never present Python alone; the Go experience is what separates this profile from the much larger Python-backend pool in both Tel Aviv and NYC.
3. **PostgreSQL and SQL in production.** Mandatory at ranks 2, 3, 8, 9, 13 and 14. Specify production ownership — schema design and query performance — not familiarity.
4. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** This has now fully crossed over from differentiator to **stated requirement**: Workday/Paradox requires production LLM products, Lendbuzz requires critical review of AI-generated code, Salesforce lists RAG and agentic workflows, Appdome requires AI tooling in testing, Workiz and PAPAYA require hands-on AI coding agents, and Garner Health and Flatiron name Claude Code directly. Frame it as **evaluation and production integration**, never as model research.

**De-emphasise:**

- **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Real signal for communication, mentoring and technical explanation — and worth one line in that framing — but never positioned as, or allowed to blur into, professional software-engineering tenure. Several reqs here have hard year bars; an ambiguous timeline invites the worst reading.
- **Academic machine-learning and data-science research.** It pulls toward the data-scientist and research-scientist pipelines, which this brief explicitly excludes and where a 2–3-year profile competes poorly. Keep the AI story anchored to **production systems and evaluation**.
- **Any seniority-signalling language.** Ranks 4 (Upwind) and 10 (Flytrex) sit next to team-lead or staff openings at the same companies. Phrasing that implies organisational leadership reframes the candidate against a pipeline they will lose to. "Owned end-to-end" — yes; "led the team" — no.
- **Frontend breadth as a headline.** Useful at ranks 3 and 11 where frontend is explicitly part of the req, and at the Workiz/Stigg tier. In backend-only applications it dilutes the specialist framing that makes this profile competitive.
