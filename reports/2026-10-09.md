## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specialising in **Go and Python on Kubernetes**, with production ownership of globally distributed, high-throughput services — and full-stack reach in TypeScript/React when a product needs it.*

Three concrete proof points, in this order, because they are what today's postings actually ask for:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the differentiator. It answers the Kubernetes, distributed-systems, concurrency, gRPC and internal-developer-platform lines at Appdome, Upwind, Rise, Zero Networks, Vercel and Garner, and almost no 2–3-year candidate can claim it.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** — answers every "data-intensive", "message queues", "pipelines" and "at scale" requirement (Upwind, DoiT, Stigg, Affirm).
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation plus text analytics/classification/summarisation** — now a *requirement* rather than a bonus in a majority of these postings (Appdome's GPT-based test validation, Workiz's AI-tooling fluency, Garner's Claude Code stack, Stigg's agentic development).

Pair these with **root-cause analysis and production debugging**, which appears as an explicit requirement in most postings and is independently evidenced.

**What to de-emphasise:**
* **Freelance/vocational teaching years.** Keep them as one line evidencing mentoring and communication; never let them read as engineering tenure, or the 2–3-year story becomes muddled and invites a seniority mismatch.
* **"Generic full-stack developer."** Node.js-first postings (Workiz, Stigg, Kela, Nagomi, Monogoto) are the weakest part of this market for this profile — leading with full-stack breadth puts the candidate into a pool where Node depth is the screen.
* **Frontend/CSS craft.** React and TypeScript belong in the story as *backend-owner-who-can-ship-UI*, not as a frontend specialism.
* **Academic ML/data-science research as a headline.** Valuable as corroboration on AI-platform roles (Appdome Identity & Reputation), but leading with it risks being routed to data-science or research reqs, which are excluded.
* **Seniority inflation.** Do not apply as Senior. The 2+ and 3+ year, explicitly mid-level postings are where this profile wins; the 5+ year postings (At-Bay, Melio, Nagomi, Kela, Apono, Drivenets, Guideline, Monogoto, Unframe, all verified this run) are where it loses on the first screen.

*Reached and verified:* Indeed Israel (multiple queries across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ra'anana, Ramat Gan, Petah Tikva, Kfar Saba and Israel-wide; full job descriptions pulled for every shortlisted role); company career pages on Comeet-on-domain (**Appdome** full board, **Upwind** full board, **Workiz**, **Kela**, **Nagomi**); Greenhouse (**DoiT/doitintl** board, **Unframe** board, **At-Bay**, **Similarweb** via aggregator resolution); **jobs.venrock.com** VC portfolio board (Zero Networks); **team8.vc** portfolio pages; **freehire.me** (four pages of Israel backend plus Israel full-stack, with original apply links resolved to source); **workopia.io**; **jobs.secrettelaviv.com** (57 listings); **topstartups.io**; **shaharpolak.com** hiring directory.

*Blocked or unusable:*
* **Comeet's own hosted boards** (`comeet.com/jobs/<company>/...`) are JavaScript-rendered and returned empty templates for **Coralogix, CommIT, Infinidat, Cognyte and Zero Networks**. Comeet postings were only reachable where a company embeds them server-side on its own domain (Appdome, Upwind, Workiz, Kela, Fullpath, Cycode, Apono, Coralogix) — so the Comeet-only segment of the Israeli market is under-covered.
* **Glassdoor** — HTTP 403 on every attempt.
* **startup.jobs** Israel — HTTP 403.
* **devjobs.co.il** — login wall; no listings retrievable. This is a meaningful Israeli-market gap.
* **LinkedIn Jobs** — not fetchable, as expected; reached only indirectly through search snippets, which were never treated as verification.
* **builtin.com/jobs/mena/israel** — the Israel location filter is ignored server-side and returns U.S. listings; Israeli Built In roles were only reachable via direct job URLs.
* **topstartups.io** and **freehire.me** paginate behind JavaScript "show more" controls, so only the first pages of each were enumerated (154 and 185+ listings respectively remained unread).
* Not budgeted this run: **Wellfound**, **Startup Nation Central**, and most individual Israeli VC portfolio boards beyond Venrock and Team8 (Aleph's board 404'd on the path tried).

**New York City — searched actively, 3 roles included (2 NYC-office, 1 remote-US).**

*Reached:* Built In NYC (mid-level and entry-level dev/engineering boards, 38 listings enumerated, individual postings fetched); Built In national backend board; Indeed NYC; ZipRecruiter (mid-level NYC backend with Go/Python/Kubernetes skills filter); Greenhouse postings reached directly.

*Failed the inclusion gate, with reasons* — recorded because the gate working is the point:
* **Raydar — Backend Software Engineer**: production **Rust** mandatory plus demonstrated trading-markets interest; two hard gaps. Excluded.
* **Hightouch — SWE, Customer Studio Backend** and **Streaming Systems**: no mandatory technologies named at all, and a $180k–$400k band that signals a bar well above the "entry level" tag. Borderline resolves to exclude.
* **XBOW — SWE Console / AI Systems**: genuine TypeScript+React+Go+PostgreSQL+AWS+Terraform overlap, but TypeScript proficiency is the core requirement and Go is listed as "or similar" — the candidate's differentiators (Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems) are not central. **Medium → excluded** under the strict U.S. gate.
* **Palantir (3 backend reqs)**: strong infrastructure match but a notoriously high bar at mid-level, and the Defense req raises an eligibility question not resolvable from available evidence. Excluded.
* **Thinking Machines Lab (Inference, Infrastructure)**: research-infrastructure bar far above 2–3 years. Excluded.
* **Garner Health — Associate Software Engineer**: requires Spring 2026 graduation or current enrolment; a new-grad req, which is an excluded category. The **Software Engineer II** req at the same company is the correct target and is at rank 2.
* **ZipRecruiter NYC results** (Capital One ML Engineer 4, J.P. Morgan Databricks, BlackRock Aladdin Associate, CrowdStrike Cloud SDET, Assembled Security Engineer): none is a hands-on backend/platform role matching the profile. Excluded.
* **Vercel caveat, stated plainly:** included at rank 8, but Built In shows "2 Locations" unnamed and quotes the San Francisco pay band. NYC anchoring rests on the NYC board listing plus the posting's NY anchor-day clause — not on an explicit "New York" location field. Confirm before applying.

**Washington, DC — searched actively, 0 roles included.** This is a real result, not an unsearched market.

*Reached:* Indeed DC/NoVA/Maryland (backend Python/distributed systems); targeted web search across DC proper, Arlington, McLean and the Maryland suburbs for Go/Python/Kubernetes backend roles at the 2-year level; `builtindc.com` attempted and returned **HTTP 404** (the site appears to have no live dev/engineering board at that path), which is itself a coverage gap.

*Why every DC candidate failed the gate:*
* **Security clearance or federal eligibility dominates the DC market.** Verified examples: VTG Defense SME Software Engineer (Chantilly), Markon Software Engineer Level 3 (Fort Meade), Data Surge Senior Databricks Engineer (explicit Secret clearance), Spatial Front API Backend (Public Trust), CGI Arlington Python/API (TS, and TS/SCI with CI Poly for the senior req), GDIT full-stack, Zachary Piper backend (explicitly "CLEARED"), Deloitte Rosslyn back-end (TS **and** 5+ years). Clearance eligibility cannot be established from the Candidate Reference, so these are correctly excluded rather than speculated on.
* **The few non-cleared DC leads failed on fit or freshness:** Press Ganey Senior Software Engineer (technical-lead scope, above profile), Comcast ML Engineer GoLang (ML-centred rather than backend product engineering), Manifold Industries Forward Deployed Engineer (customer-facing), Foundation Stack AI Full Stack Developer McLean (verified as a listing but no retrievable requirement detail at the 2-year level), and an Administrative Office of the Courts DevSecOps role explicitly marked "clearance not required" but with an application deadline of **25 May 2026** — long expired, and a federal IT/DevSecOps role rather than product engineering.
* Net: the DC metro's non-cleared, hands-on, 0–3-year backend/platform segment produced **nothing** that would deserve a High rating. Zero is reported rather than padded.

**Connector performance, for next run's budgeting:**
* **Indeed** — the most productive connector for Israel by a wide margin; full job descriptions with reliable posting dates. Returned nothing useful for NYC on Go/Python/Kubernetes queries and only clearance-gated defence roles for DC.
* **Indeed `get_resume`** — succeeded; corroborating only.
* **ZipRecruiter** — ran for NYC; five results, none passing the gate. Low yield as expected.
* **Dice** — **not called this run.** Budget went to Israel depth and to NYC/DC web verification instead; it is a known weak source for Israel and the U.S. gate is strict enough that Dice's volume rarely clears it. Recorded as an honest omission.
* **Gmail** — used for draft delivery only.

**One integrity note:** a Workopia listing field (Placerlabs) and a freehire apply link (Team8/Monogoto) each contained embedded content that did not belong to the job data — a work-mode claim contradicting the posting body, and another person's email address and candidate-tracking identifiers in URL parameters. Both were treated as untrusted data: the contradicting claim was disregarded in favour of the posting text, the personal identifiers were stripped, and neither role appears in the table.
