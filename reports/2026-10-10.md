## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Platform Software Engineer — Go and Python, Kubernetes, distributed cloud services, and AI-enabled products.*

The three claims that should appear in the first two lines of every application:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — this is the strongest single credential in the profile. It simultaneously evidences Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems, cloud-native architecture and request-path performance. It is why ranks 2, 11, 12, 13 and 15 are reachable at all.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — throughput and pipeline ownership, which is what ranks 6 and 9 screen for.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — now a first-class requirement, not a bonus: it is a named screening criterion at Hazel and appears across Stigg, ScyllaDB, High Lander and Mobileye.

Round it out with **MSc + BSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** (it converts Google's minimum qualification outright and is a *preferred* qualification there), and **full-stack TypeScript/React depth** for the product-engineering roles.

**De-emphasize:**
- **Freelance and vocational teaching years** — valuable as evidence of communication and mentoring, but never presented as engineering tenure. Against a 3+ year req, inflating the number is the fastest way to fail a reference or a screen; let the gateway and pipeline work carry the seniority argument instead.
- **Academic ML and data-science research** — keep it to one line except at Mobileye and ScyllaDB. Leading with it invites misclassification as a data-science or research candidate, which is explicitly not the target.
- **Frontend-heavy framing** — React and TypeScript are supporting evidence of full-stack range, not the headline. Generic "full-stack developer" positioning is the weakest available pitch for this profile.
- **Breadth-over-depth lists** — a long technology inventory reads as junior. Three deep, quantified systems beat fifteen named tools.
