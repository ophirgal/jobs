## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Platform Engineer — Go and Python, Kubernetes, high-throughput distributed services, with production generative-AI experience.*

Three concrete assets should appear in the first two lines of every application:

1. **"Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day."** This single line is doing most of the work in this report — it is what qualifies the candidate for Google's infrastructure minimum (ranks 1, 12), Temporal's proxy and distributed-systems reqs (4, 9), Yext's retrieval-and-serving scope (6), and Upwind's and Claroty's gateway work. Quantify it every time.
2. **"Production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day."** The throughput proof for Mobileye (10), DoiT (5, 13) and Taboola (15).
3. **MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9).** Not a nice-to-have — it is the *mechanism* that lowers Google's bar from two years to one on ranks 1 and 12, and it satisfies Taboola's degree requirement outright.

**Position as mid-level, explicitly.** The record is 2–3 years. The roles that convert here are 1+, 1.5+, 2+ and "entry level / no years stated"; the 3+ year roles (DoiT, Mobileye, Upwind, Taboola, Rise) are reachable precisely because the technical overlap is unusually specific. Do not apply to Senior, Staff or Lead reqs — this run independently excluded Melio, Similarweb (×3), Apiiro, Cymulate, WalkMe, all six Cato Networks reqs, Vercel's "eve", and ZoomInfo on 4+ to 6+ year bars alone. Chasing those is where the time goes to waste.

**De-emphasize:** freelance/vocational teaching years (keep them as one line evidencing mentoring and communication — never let them read as engineering tenure, and never let the total years read as more than 2–3); pure frontend and CSS work; academic ML research framed as research (reframe it as *AI-system evaluation and production ML support*, which is what ranks 5, 10, 11 and 15 actually reward); and any DevOps-flavored self-description — every target here is a software-engineering role, and "DevOps" framing routes the résumé to the wrong pile.

## 🔹 Search Gaps — what was and wasn't reached

**Honest accounting of this run.** All three markets were genuinely searched; the coverage was not equal, and the reasons differ by market.

**Israel — searched comprehensively (primary market, and it produced 10 of 15 roles).**
- ✅ **Greenhouse board APIs: ~150 company slugs probed**, returning live job sets for Similarweb, Rubrik, JFrog, Riskified, Fireblocks, Melio, Via, Taboola, Orca, Torq, Axonius, BigID, VAST, Datarails, Island, Descope, Transmit Security, Forter, Cymulate, SafeBreach, Sweet Security, Apiiro, Cato Networks, Yotpo, Lightrun, DoiT, Imubit, Augury, Cybereason, Payoneer, Bringg. - ✅ **Indeed connector**: eight distinct searches across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ramat Gan, Petah Tikva, Central District and Israel-wide, on Go/Python/Kubernetes/full-stack/distributed-systems/Golang keyword sets.
- ✅ **Comeet-hosted boards** re-verified individually (Appdome, Upwind, Zero Networks ×2, Workiz) — which is how both Zero Networks closures were caught.
- ✅ **Official company career pages** fetched directly for Mobileye, Taboola, Cato Networks, Workiz, Upwind, Appdome, and Team8's Claroty listing.
- ❌ **Could not reach:** **LinkedIn Jobs** (blocks automated fetch, as always — worked around via WebSearch plus company-site pivots, never cited as verification). **Glassdoor** returned heavily on every WebSearch but is an aggregator with unreliable dates and was used only as a lead source, never as verification; several roles it surfaced (Hyro, aiOla, PVML, NimbleWay, Kendago, Varonis, groundcover, Rubrik Israel) could not be confirmed on a first-party board within budget and were therefore **excluded rather than listed**. **Startup Nation Central**, **Secret Tel Aviv**, **freehire.me**, and Israeli-VC portfolio boards beyond Team8 and Venrock were not successfully enumerated. **Lever's API failed outright** this run — two connection resets through the egress proxy (`ws_closed_mid_exchange`), so ~25 Lever-hosted Israeli companies (Pentera, Cycode, Legit, BigPanda, Logz.io, Aporia, Tenable, Rapid7 and others) went unsearched. - ⚠️ **Structural finding, not a gap:** the Israeli market at 2–3 years is genuinely thin. Of every Israel req opened this run, the overwhelming majority were 4+, 5+ or 6+ years — Melio (5+), Similarweb Backend (5+), Similarweb Agent Platform (5+), Similarweb Backend-with-AI (5+), Apiiro (5+), Cymulate (5+), WalkMe (5+), and **all six Cato Networks reqs** (4+/4+/5+/6+).
