## ⚠️ U.S. eligibility note — stated honestly

The candidate is a **U.S. citizen requiring no visa sponsorship**, which clears the single most common U.S. gate. It is a material advantage twice in this table: at **Garner Health**, which explicitly does not sponsor, and at **Gallatin**, where U.S. citizenship is a hard contractual requirement that screens out most of its own applicant pool. Three caveats are recorded rather than assumed away:

1. **Residency / relocation.** Garner (NYC, 3 office days), Yext (Rosslyn, VA, in-person) and Gallatin (DC, on-site) require physically being in-metro. The three remote-US roles (Temporal ×2, Affirm) are "Remote — United States," which normally means U.S.-resident payroll. Nothing in the Candidate Reference establishes current or intended U.S. residence. **This is an unknown eligibility risk, not a disqualifier** — flagged per role rather than silently resolved either way.
2. **Security clearance — Gallatin only.** The posting says the role "may require the ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance" and that the hire "must be able to work in a classified environment when necessary." Citizenship is established; **clearance eligibility is not**, and no evidence in this run speaks to it. Because the condition is conditional ("may require") rather than a precondition of hire, the role was included with the risk named. Every DC posting that required an *already-held* TS/SCI or CI-poly clearance was excluded outright — several surfaced in the ZipRecruiter DC sweep and all were dropped on that basis.
3. **Indeed profile signal.** The resume retrieved this run carries **"Willing to Relocate: No"** and a "Minimum Salary: GBP 2,000" field. Both read as stale, unmaintained preference fields rather than authoritative statements, so neither was used to exclude U.S. roles — but the relocation flag is the reason the U.S. section was held to six High-tier roles rather than expanded further, and it is worth correcting on the Indeed profile if U.S. roles are genuinely in scope.

## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes, with production ownership of high-throughput distributed services.* The three artifacts that do the most work, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day** — this single item satisfies the "large-scale infrastructure / distributed systems" line at Google, the proxy-and-gateway brief at Temporal and Claroty, the request-path framing at Upwind, and the scale argument that counters every 3+ and 4+ year bar in the table. Lead with it everywhere.
2. **A production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day** — the direct answer to Affirm's streaming and event-driven brief, Gallatin's "real-time, batch, structured and unstructured sources," and DoiT's ETL throughput language.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation plus text analytics, classification and summarisation** — the differentiator at Yext (search and chat), Gallatin (AI-driven decisions), Pipl (daily AI-assisted development as a hard requirement) and ScyllaDB (AI-powered end-to-end).

Add the **MSc in CS (UMD, 3.9 GPA)** explicitly and early in every Google application — it is not a credential flourish there, it is the mechanism that halves the stated experience requirement. Keep **full-stack TypeScript/React** as a stated secondary capability: it is what makes DoiT, Garner, Pipl and ScyllaDB fit, but it should never be the headline.

**De-emphasise:** the title "Full-Stack Developer" as an identity, and frontend work framed as UI or styling — several excluded roles this run were frontend-dominant and the profile reads stronger as backend-first. Do not foreground freelance teaching tenure as engineering experience; convert it instead into the mentoring, communication and technical-explanation evidence that Gallatin ("supporting the onboarding and mentoring of new teammates"), Pipl and Garner all ask for explicitly. Avoid presenting academic ML research as a path toward research-scientist roles — it is supporting evidence for AI-*product* engineering, not a research pitch. Do not reach for Senior, Staff or Lead framing: every High-tier role in this table is reachable precisely because it is scoped at 0–3 years.
