## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes, with production experience in globally distributed, high-throughput cloud services and AI-enabled products.*

Three concrete proof points, in this order, and keep them in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day.** This is your single most differentiating line. Controller and operator development is genuinely uncommon at 2–3 years of experience, and it is the specific thing that makes Temporal's proxy role, Appdome's Internal Platforms role, Google's platform-infrastructure roles and Garner's Kubernetes shop all legible as realistic targets. Lead every application with it.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day.** Your evidence for scale and for data-intensive backend work. This is what answers "has this person operated anything real?"
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems.** In this specific set of openings, AI experience is no longer a bonus — ScyllaDB, Garner, Google Database Conversion, SimilarWeb, Stigg, Appdome and Yext *all* name AI tooling, agents or evaluation in their requirements. Frame yours as **engineering** work (building and evaluating production AI systems, root-cause analysis of model-driven errors), not as research interest. That framing is what separates you from applicants who have only prompt-engineered.

Add, as supporting depth rather than headline: full-stack capability with TypeScript and React where the role asks for backend ownership across the stack (Stigg, Taboola, Garner); MSc in Computer Science, which is a stated qualifying or preferred credential at Google (twice in this report) and at PIPL; and strong debugging and root-cause analysis, which several of these postings call out explicitly.

**De-emphasize:**
- **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Do not let them read as software-engineering tenure — that invites a correction and damages credibility at exactly the moment a reviewer is checking your years. Present teaching separately and briefly, as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical explanation. It belongs in the "collaborates and communicates well" column that Appdome, SimilarWeb, Stigg and DIRECTV all ask for, not in the experience count.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** Against these openings it pulls you toward data-science and research-scientist roles you've explicitly excluded. Mention it only where AI-system evaluation is in the job description, and frame it as production engineering.
- **Frontend-only framing.** Never lead with React or CSS. Your leverage in this market is backend and platform; full-stack is a secondary capability that broadens you, not your identity.
- **Breadth-for-its-own-sake.** Several of these roles want depth in one thing. Go/Python plus Kubernetes plus distributed systems is a sharper and more hireable story than a long comma-separated technology list.
