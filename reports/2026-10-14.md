## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **"Backend / Full-Stack Software Engineer — Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with AI-product experience."**

Open every application with the two artifacts that do the most work, because they are concrete, quantified and unusual at this level:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this single line simultaneously evidences Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems, cloud-native design and production ownership. It is the candidate's strongest asset and should appear in the first two sentences, not buried in a bullet list.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day** — establishes real throughput and data-pipeline engineering, which is what "scalable backend" means in practice.

Then layer: generative-AI system evaluation and improvement (a live differentiator — Garner, ScyllaDB, Lendbuzz, Taboola and Appdome all explicitly name AI tooling in their requirements, which is unusual and plays directly to this background); root-cause analysis and production debugging; React/TypeScript full-stack delivery as *breadth supporting backend ownership*; and the **UMD MSc at 3.9 GPA**, which is load-bearing rather than decorative at Google, where it converts the "2 years" minimum into "1 year with an advanced degree."

**De-emphasize:** freelance teaching years — present them as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical explanation, never as professional software-engineering tenure (conflating the two invites the exact seniority mismatch that disqualifies a candidate at screening). Also de-emphasize: academic ML/data-science research when applying to product-engineering roles (it reads as research-track and can misroute the application toward roles explicitly out of scope); pure frontend and CSS work; and any framing as "generalist full-stack developer," which understates the backend and platform depth that is the actual selling point.

**Do not apply as** Senior, Staff, Architect or Tech Lead. The evidence from this run is unambiguous: twelve Israeli roles were excluded today at 4+ to 6+ years. Applying above band wastes the strongest asset, which is being clearly *above* the bar on a 2+ yr req rather than visibly below it on a 5+ yr one.
