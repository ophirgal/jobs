## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specialising in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production full-stack range and generative-AI product experience.** Three concrete artefacts should appear in the first six lines of any application: **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day** (platform depth), **production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** (scale), and **improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** (the differentiator — it appears in Riskified's LLM-evidence work, ScyllaDB's AI-assisted development, Upwind's AI Engineer req, Comet's Opik, Tailscale's Aperture and Yext's search-and-chat).

Add one line on the **MSc in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA**. It is not decorative here: Google's minimum qualification explicitly converts an advanced degree into a year of experience, and Lendbuzz names a top-tier degree with honours as a selection criterion.

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years as though they were engineering tenure — quote 2–3 years of professional software engineering, cleanly, and let the teaching stand separately as evidence of communication and mentoring. Also de-emphasise frontend-first framing (it reads as a UI engineer and costs the backend roles that make up most of this table), generic "full-stack developer" phrasing, and any claim to model *training* or research-scientist work — the credible AI claim is evaluation, improvement and production integration, and several reqs this run draw exactly that line.
