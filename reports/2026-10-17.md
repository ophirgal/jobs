## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / platform engineer — Go and Python, Kubernetes, and scalable cloud services, with full-stack reach and AI-product experience.**

The three claims that carried every strong match in this run, in order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the single highest-leverage sentence in the profile and should open every application and every recruiter message. It is simultaneously evidence of Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems, production scale and infrastructure ownership — five requirement lines in one claim — and it is what makes a 3+ or 4+ year bar negotiable.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics, plus generative-AI system evaluation.** Use this wherever a posting mentions batch pipelines, throughput, AI agents, LLM features or AI-assisted development — Affirm's Batch Developer Experience, Google's Database Conversion AI-agent work, ScyllaDB's AI-tooling expectation, Appdome's GPT-based test validation.
3. **MSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland (3.9 GPA).** Not background colour — a *scored qualification*. Both Google Israel reqs cut the minimum experience bar in half for an advanced degree, and the Cross-Cloud Observability req lists a Master's as preferred. State it early on anything Google-adjacent or research-flavoured.

**De-emphasise:**

* **Freelance / vocational teaching years.** Keep them visible as evidence of communication and mentoring — they are a genuine asset in interviews and in the Appdome and Garner cross-functional framing — but never let them read as engineering tenure. Blurring the two invites a recruiter to classify the profile as career-changer, which is strictly worse than a clean 2–3 years of focused backend work.
* **Academic ML / data-science research.** Mention only where a posting asks for AI or ML (Google Database Conversion, ScyllaDB). Leading with it on a backend req risks being routed to data-science pipelines, where a PhD-heavy pool competes.
* **Frontend depth.** React and TypeScript are real and belong in the stack list for Garner, Taboola, ScyllaDB and Workiz — but as *full-stack reach from a backend base*, never as the headline. "Full-stack developer" is a materially weaker position for this profile than "backend engineer who ships product end-to-end."
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake.** Java, C# and Scala adjacency is worth a line in a cover note when a posting is Java-primary (Taboola, Yext's secondary language). It is not worth diluting the Go/Python/Kubernetes headline.
