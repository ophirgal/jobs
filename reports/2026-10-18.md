## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / platform engineer — Go and Python, Kubernetes, and scalable cloud services, with full-stack reach and AI-product experience.**

The three claims that carried every strong match in this run, in order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** The highest-leverage sentence in the profile, and it should open every application and recruiter message. It is simultaneously evidence of Go, Kubernetes, distributed systems, production scale and infrastructure ownership — five requirement lines in one claim. For **Temporal's proxy role specifically, it is not an analogy but the same work**, and it is what makes a 3+ or 4+ year bar negotiable elsewhere.

3. **MSc in Computer Science, University of Maryland (3.9 GPA).** A *scored qualification*, not background colour: both Google Israel reqs in the table cut the minimum experience bar in half for an advanced degree. State it early on anything Google-adjacent.

**De-emphasise:**

* **Freelance / vocational teaching years.** Keep them visible as evidence of communication and mentoring — genuinely useful in interviews and in the Appdome and Garner cross-functional framing — but never let them read as engineering tenure. Blurring the two invites a recruiter to classify the profile as career-changer, which is strictly worse than a clean 2–3 years of focused backend work. *(Note: the Indeed résumé on file leads with two Vocational Training Instructor entries, the most recent dated September 2024–Present, above the software engineering roles. That ordering actively works against this positioning and is worth reordering at profile.indeed.com.)*
* **Academic ML / data-science research.** Mention only where a posting asks for AI or ML (Google Personalized Identity, AppsFlyer's AI Assistant). Leading with it on a backend req risks routing into data-science pipelines against a PhD-heavy pool.
* **Frontend depth.** React and TypeScript are real and belong in the stack list for Garner, Ramp Core Product, Riskified, ScyllaDB and Taboola — but as *full-stack reach from a backend base*, never as the headline. "Full-stack developer" is a materially weaker position for this profile than "backend engineer who ships product end-to-end."
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake.** Java, C#, Scala and Kotlin adjacency is worth a line in a cover note when a posting is Java- or Scala-primary (Yext's secondary language, Riskified, AppsFlyer, Taboola). It is not worth diluting the Go/Python/Kubernetes headline.

## 🔹 Search Gaps

That recovered **Ramp** (requirements unreadable in two prior runs), **Temporal** (experience bar unreadable in three prior runs — it turns out no bar is stated), **Riskified** (403 last run) and **Comet** (404 for two runs). All URLs in this report were reached by retrieval, none constructed from assumption; board slugs that returned non-200 were discarded without claiming anything from them.
