## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes and AWS, with production ownership of high-throughput distributed services and hands-on experience building and evaluating AI-enabled product features.*

Three concrete proof points, in this order, every time:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — this is the differentiating claim and it is unusual at 2–3 years. Every platform, proxy, gateway, infrastructure and internal-tools req in this report is won or lost on it.
2. **Production application processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — the throughput and pipeline story; it answers "scale" without requiring Spark or Airflow on the résumé.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems; text analytics, classification, sentiment and summarisation** — this is the fastest-appreciating asset in both markets. Four of the fourteen tabled reqs name production LLM work explicitly.

Add **MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9)** wherever a req states a years minimum with an advanced-degree reduction — at Google that literally halves the bar, and it is the reason rank 13 is applicable at all.

**De-emphasise:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Present them once, as evidence of mentoring and technical communication — never in a way that reads as engineering tenure. A recruiter who perceives padded years discounts the whole résumé.
* **Academic ML/data-science research.** It helps only on reqs that name AI-product or data work. On a backend/platform req it invites mis-routing into data-science or research pipelines, which the candidate does not want.
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake framing.** "Full-stack developer" undersells this profile badly; "backend engineer with full-stack reach" is the correct ordering. Frontend/CSS work should never lead.
* **Senior framing.** Do not apply upward. Ranks 1–10 are all reachable on stated requirements; the leverage is in being an unambiguous fit at mid-level, not a stretch at senior.
