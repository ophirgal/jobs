## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / Platform Software Engineer — Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production AI-system experience.**

The three assets that actually differentiate this candidate in both markets, in order:

1. **Kubernetes controller development for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day.** This is the headline. It is rare at 2–3 years of experience, and it is the single line that makes Porter, Ramp Production Engineering, Temporal Cloud Enablement, Northwood and Google Chronicle all read as plausible rather than aspirational. Put it in the first sentence of every application.
2. **High-throughput production data processing** — 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics — paired with **generative-AI system evaluation**. Nearly every req verified this run (Ramp Credit, AppsFlyer 2938, Appdome, ScyllaDB, RISE, Similarweb, Garner) now carries an LLM, agent or AI-tooling component. The candidate can speak to *evaluating* AI systems, not just calling them, which is the scarcer skill.
3. **Independently built internal engineering tools.** This converts directly into the developer-productivity and platform reqs — Affirm's Batch Developer Experience, Ramp Production Engineering, Northwood's entire job description.

Frame full-stack as **backend ownership that extends through the UI** (React/TypeScript at Qualtrics), never as generic full-stack: four of the five Israeli roles that were excluded on fit, and the one U.S. role excluded, skewed frontend or framework-specific.

**What to de-emphasise:**

* **Do not present the freelance teaching years as engineering tenure.** Professional software engineering is ~2–3 years; teaching is evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth, and belongs in a separate section. Conflating them invites a credibility problem in a screen that will be checked.
* **Do not lead with the academic ML/data-science research.** It pulls toward research and data-science reqs that are explicitly out of scope and that this profile would lose. Keep the **MSc** prominent (it has direct mechanical value — it halves Google's experience requirement) but keep the research itself as a footnote.
* **Do not over-index on text analytics, classification and sentiment analysis.** These read as applied-NLP/data-science signals, and none of the fifteen roles here is buying them. The generative-AI *evaluation and infrastructure* framing travels much further.
* **Do not claim Senior.** Every role in this table is mid-level by design, and three of the strongest (Porter 1+, Affirm 1.5+, Garner 2+) are reachable precisely because the band is honest.
* **Do not volunteer Rust, Scala, Java or Kotlin depth.** Where those are mandatory (Coralogix, Wiz, Cato, Taboola, Cycode) the role was excluded; where they are optional (Northwood, RISE, ERGO NEXT, Upwind), the Go/Python strength is the better argument.
