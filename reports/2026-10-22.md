## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / platform engineer who builds control planes and high-throughput services — Go and Python on Kubernetes and AWS — with production generative-AI experience and a full-stack TypeScript/React range.**

The three claims that do the most work, in order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day (SAP).** This is the differentiator. Say "control plane", "controller", "reconciliation" and "orchestration" — not "worked with Kubernetes". It is what makes MongoDB Atlas Clusters, Ramp Production Engineering, Stigg Metering, Upwind, Google Chronicle SOAR and Porter all legible as the same job.
2. **100k+ documents/day production pipeline plus generative-AI improvement and evaluation (Qualtrics).** This covers the throughput story and the AI story at once, and it is the live requirement at Palo Alto (Cortex Agentix), Centrical, Garner (Claude Code) and Stash ("Agent Conductor"). Frame AI work as *production systems engineering with evaluation rigor* — never as research.
3. **Independently built internal engineering tools, plus root-cause analysis and debugging.** This is the explicit ask at Affirm (Batch Developer Experience), Appdome (Internal Platforms), Stigg (DevEx), Centrical and Stash (testing/debugging/profiling), and it is what justifies a mid-level engineer being trusted with platform scope.

**Supporting, not leading:** the **MSc + 3.9 GPA from University of Maryland** — deploy it where it changes eligibility (the Google "1 year with an advanced degree" clause, Lendbuzz's GPA line) rather than as a general credential.

**De-emphasize:**

* **Years.** With 2–3 years against reqs that mostly say 3+, the number loses and the scope wins. Lead every application with the systems owned, not the tenure.
* **Freelance and academic teaching.** Keep it as one line signalling communication and mentoring. Never let it read as engineering tenure — several of these employers will discount the whole CV if the timeline looks inflated.
* **Frontend breadth.** React/TypeScript is an *enabler* for full-stack ownership (MongoDB, Garner, Stigg Full Stack). Do not lead with it; the pure-frontend and ad-format roles found today are exactly what to avoid.
* **ML and data-science research.** Mentioning it invites comparison to PhD research profiles. "Evaluating and improving production generative-AI systems" is the framing that wins; "machine-learning research" is not.
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake stack lists.** Depth in Go/Python/Kubernetes beats a long technology inventory with every reviewer in this set.
