## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Full-Stack Engineer — Go, Python, Kubernetes, and scalable cloud services, with production AI-system experience.* Not "full-stack developer."

The three claims that carry every application in this set:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day (SAP).** This is the differentiator. Use the control-plane vocabulary — *controller, reconciliation, orchestration, control plane* — not "worked with Kubernetes." It is what makes Porter, MongoDB Atlas Clusters, Stigg Metering, Upwind, ScyllaDB and Salt Security Edge all legible as one job the candidate has already done.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day (Qualtrics).** The throughput number is what converts "2–3 years" from a limitation into evidence of scale. Lead with it at AppsFlyer, Garner Health and Taboola.
3. **Full-stack ownership from HTTP layer to UI — TypeScript, React, REST APIs, PostgreSQL.** Pinecone, Taboola, Stigg and Baseten each ask for exactly this in their own words. Quote their phrasing back.
4. **Evaluating and improving generative-AI systems.** In 2026 this is now a *stated requirement* rather than a bonus — Palo Alto Networks (LLM prompting and context management), Pinecone Applied AI (quality/latency/cost evaluation), AppsFlyer (multi-agent orchestration and MCP) and Appdome (GPT-based test generation) all ask for it. Say **"evaluate and improve"**, which is engineering, not **"used an LLM API"**, which is not.

**De-emphasise:**

* **Freelance teaching years.** They are genuine evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — put them in the interview as *why I explain systems well and mentor effectively*, never in the experience total. Conflating them invites a credibility challenge that costs more than the years gain.
* **Pure frontend and CSS work.** Every role here wants backend ownership. Frontend is the second sentence, never the first.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** It reads as a signal of wanting a research role, which none of these are. The exception: use it as *evidence of judgment about model behaviour* when discussing AI-system evaluation.
* **The MSc as a general credential.** Deploy it where it changes eligibility or seniority framing — not as a line item. With the Google req now unverifiable, its highest-leverage use this week is the PIPL-style *"B.Sc./M.Sc."* framing and as the reason a 3-year engineer reasons like a 5-year one.
