**Washington, DC produced zero included roles for the fourth consecutive run, and the DC role carried over from yesterday is being dropped.** The Appian Senior Software Engineer (McLean, VA) re-verified live, but on a stricter reading it fails the U.S. gate: the title is Senior, the posting requires the ability to obtain a DoD Tier 3 Secret clearance for IL4/IL5 access, and the work is on-site in McLean.
