## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-throughput cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.*

Concretely, the top of the CV and every outreach note should carry these four artifacts, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — this is the differentiator. It reads as control-plane and platform engineering, which is exactly what ranks 1, 10, 11 and 15 are buying, and it is rare at the 2–3 year mark.
2. **Production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — the scale evidence for every "high-throughput", "distributed data", or "event-driven" requirement (ranks 5, 7, 8, 9, 14).
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation and improvement** — no longer a nice-to-have. It is a *stated requirement* at Appdome, Taboola, Ramp Credit, RISE and Pinecone, and a mandatory daily-workflow item at Taboola. Name the evaluation methodology, not just the exposure.
4. **Full-stack React/TypeScript ownership** (Qualtrics) — the second half of ranks 3, 12 and 13. Keep it present but subordinate; it should read as range, not as the centre of gravity.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance and academic teaching as experience.** It is strong evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — mention it as such, in one line — but never let it imply years of full-time engineering. Several reqs here have hard floors, and a reviewer who suspects padding discounts the whole CV.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** It invites misreading as a research-scientist profile, which is the wrong funnel for all fifteen of these roles. Convert it into *applied AI evaluation* language instead.
- **Text analytics, sentiment and summarisation as a specialism.** Useful supporting colour at Cato and Taboola; a liability if it makes the candidate look like an NLP specialist rather than a backend engineer.
- **Breadth-for-its-own-sake stack lists.** With a 2–3 year record, four deep artifacts beat fourteen shallow technologies. Drop anything not used in production.
