## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production AI-system experience.**

The three sentences that should appear in every application:
1. **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the single most differentiating fact in the profile and it is doing the work in roles #1, #4, #9, #10 and #15.
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics**, with Python and full-stack React/TypeScript ownership. This covers the full-stack and data-pipeline reqs (#3, #11, #12, #13, #14).
3. **Professional evaluation and improvement of generative-AI systems.** Five of the fifteen postings now make AI-augmented development or AI-system work an explicit requirement rather than a bonus — Taboola names Claude Code and Cursor outright, DoiT expects daily AI-augmented work, Appdome's team is building AI test generation. This has moved from differentiator to table stakes in Israeli reqs, and the candidate is on the right side of it.

**De-emphasize:** freelance teaching years as professional engineering tenure — present them as evidence of mentoring and technical communication, which is how they read well, and keep the engineering count honest at 2–3 years. Also de-emphasize the academic ML/data-science research; against these fifteen reqs it invites mis-sorting into data-science pipelines, and none of these roles are research positions. Do not apply above level: Senior, Staff and Architect titles are a bad use of applications at this stage, and today's re-read of Affirm is the reminder that a "II" in a title can still mean five years.
