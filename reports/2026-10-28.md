## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer with Go, Python and Kubernetes at production scale, who ships full-stack when the product needs it**. The two artefacts that do the most work are concrete and should appear in the first three lines of every application: **Kubernetes controllers in Go for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day (SAP)** and a **production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day (Qualtrics)**. Both are throughput numbers attached to systems, which is what a backend hiring manager screens for and what most 2–3-year candidates cannot supply.

Second-order differentiators, in order of usefulness for *this* set of openings: **generative-AI system evaluation** (lands at Pinecone, Braze, Taboola, Appdome — all four reqs explicitly reference AI in the product or the workflow); **debugging and root-cause analysis** (lands everywhere, and is the thing that makes a language gap survivable); the **MSc from UMD** (a real differentiator in structured loops at MongoDB and Braze, near-irrelevant at seed-stage startups).

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years — they are a communication and mentoring signal, not engineering tenure, and listing them adjacent to professional experience invites a recruiter to read the timeline wrong. Also de-emphasise breadth-for-breadth's-sake stack lists; against a 3+ req the candidate wins on depth in two languages, not on naming eight.

**One housekeeping item with real consequences:** the Indeed profile currently reads **"Willing to Relocate: No"** with a GBP salary minimum. For a U.S.-citizen candidate actively searching NYC, that setting suppresses exactly the inbound it should attract, and the currency makes the profile look mis-targeted to any U.S. recruiter who opens it. Worth five minutes at [profile.indeed.com](https://profile.indeed.com).

The structural reason DC produces nothing for this profile is worth stating once rather than rediscovering monthly: the DC-metro engineering market is dominated by government-contracting and defence employers whose postings carry clearance or citizenship-plus-clearance conditions, and the commercial remainder is thin at the mid level. Expect zero-to-one DC roles per run, and treat any DC result as a genuine find rather than evidence the search is working.
