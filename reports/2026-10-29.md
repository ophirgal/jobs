## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Platform Engineer — Go, Python, Kubernetes, distributed cloud services, AI-enabled products.*

The through-line that works for ten of the fifteen roles here is **control-plane and platform engineering**: Kubernetes controllers written in Go, in production, behind a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests per day. That one sentence is simultaneously the Porter job description, the Duolingo job description, the MongoDB Atlas Clusters job description, and the phrase Google's own posting uses ("control planes"). It should be the first line of the CV summary and the first line of every cover note. Back it with the Qualtrics throughput number (100k+ documents/day) whenever the role mentions pipelines or scale.

**Add, where the posting invites it:** professional **generative-AI evaluation** experience. This run is the first where that is a *stated mandatory requirement* rather than a bonus — ID.me asks for a "demonstrated track record of using and building with modern AI and Agentic AI tools," SailPoint calls agentic workflows "a baseline expectation," and Taboola, Yotpo and Braze all name AI-assisted development explicitly. Most mid-level applicants answer this with hobby projects; the candidate can answer it with work.

**Also carry:** the **MSc in Computer Science**. It is not decoration here — Google's minimum qualification explicitly discounts the experience requirement by a year for an advanced degree, and it differentiates in MongoDB's and Google's structured loops.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance teaching years.** They are strong evidence of communication and mentoring and belong in one line under the SAP and Qualtrics roles — never in a way that could read as padding the professional-engineering total. Several of these reqs sit at a 3+ floor against a 2–3-year record; an inflated-looking timeline invites exactly the scrutiny that loses the benefit of the doubt.
- **Frontend-first framing.** React and TypeScript are real assets at Pinecone, Vercel, Braze, Taboola and Cato, but they should appear as *full-stack ownership*, never as the headline. "Full-stack developer" is the weakest available positioning for this profile.
- **Academic ML/data-science research.** It supports the AI-systems narrative in one clause; led with, it misroutes the candidate toward research-scientist screens that the profile is not built for and that this report deliberately excludes.

**One housekeeping item, now several runs old and still unfixed:** the Indeed profile says **"Willing to Relocate: No"** and sets a **GBP 2,000 minimum salary**. With three live U.S. roles in this table (and ID.me explicitly five-days-on-site in McLean), those two fields will quietly suppress U.S. recruiter outreach and misprice the candidate on the one platform that surfaced two of this run's finds. Worth ten minutes at [profile.indeed.com](https://profile.indeed.com).
