## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend engineer specialising in **Go**, **Kubernetes** and **scalable cloud services**, with production **full-stack** capability in **React/TypeScript** and hands-on **generative-AI** engineering.*

Three concrete assets should appear in the first six lines of any application in this report:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day.** This is the most differentiating item on the profile and it is *literally* restated in the requirements of #1 (Porter), #3 (Zero Networks), #9 (Google), #13 (MongoDB) and #14 (RISE). Most mid-level backend candidates have *used* Kubernetes; writing controllers against its API is a different claim. Never bury it.
2. **Production throughput numbers — 100k+ requests/day and 100k+ documents/day.** Specific figures beat adjectives, and they are what make the AppsFlyer, RISE, Taboola and Google infrastructure reqs plausible from a ~3-year profile.
3. **Professional generative-AI system evaluation and improvement.** This run reinforces the pattern first noted on 10-29: AI tooling fluency has moved from bonus to **stated mandatory requirement**. ID.me demands a *"demonstrated track record of using and building with modern AI and Agentic AI tools"*; SailPoint calls agentic workflows *"a baseline expectation"*; Check Point, RISE, Appdome and Taboola all require AI-assisted development explicitly. Most mid-level applicants answer this with side projects. This candidate answers it with work — that asymmetry is currently the single most underused advantage in the profile.

**Add where the posting invites it:** the **UMD MSc** (a named preferred qualification at Google and SailPoint, and the route to Google's *"1 year of experience with an advanced degree"* alternative), **Protobuf/gRPC** tooling (an explicit advantage at RISE, relevant at MongoDB), and **root-cause analysis on production systems** (an explicit responsibility at Google, Appdome and RISE).

**De-emphasise:**

* **Freelance teaching years.** Position teaching as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical-explanation ability — which the Figma, Appdome and Taboola postings all reward — but never let it blur the headline count of **~2–3 years of full-time software engineering**. Inflating the number invites a senior-level screen the profile will not survive.
* **Pure frontend and CSS depth.** Full-stack breadth is an asset at #2, #10 and #11; a frontend-forward framing actively hurts everywhere else in this table.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** Relevant as context for the AI-platform roles, misleading as a headline — it attracts research-scientist screens, which are explicitly outside the target role families.
* **Java.** It appears on the Indeed résumé skills list and is the mandatory language at ControlMonkey, Taboola's SWE req and (until this week) SailPoint. Unless a posting requires it, leading with Java pulls the candidacy toward JVM-shop pipelines where the profile is weakest and away from the Go/Kubernetes positioning where it is strongest.

**Two profile-hygiene fixes, unchanged from prior runs and still unaddressed.** The Indeed profile states **"Willing to Relocate: No"** and a **GBP 2,000 minimum salary**. The relocation flag suppresses inbound for every U.S. role in this report, and a GBP-denominated figure of that size is read by U.S. and Israeli recruiter tooling as either a data error or a very low expectation. Both are a few minutes of editing at `profile.indeed.com` and both are currently costing inbound opportunities.
