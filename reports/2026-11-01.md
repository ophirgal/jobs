Build every application around three concrete, quantified artifacts, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the strongest single line in the profile and it is what distinguishes the candidate from the large pool of generic mid-level full-stack developers. It alone qualifies them for platform, infrastructure and control-plane roles (Appdome, Stigg, ScaleOps, MongoDB Atlas, Google Cloud Networking).
2. **Production pipelines processing 100k+ documents/day** — the scale credential, and the bridge to data-intensive and high-throughput requirements (DoiT, Robinhood, AppsFlyer).
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems**, with text analytics, classification, sentiment and summarisation — the genuine differentiator in a market where every posting now lists AI tooling. Lead with this for Comet, Pinecone and ID.me's AI-platform half, and mention it everywhere else as evidence of current practice rather than as a specialism.

Add the **MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9)** wherever a posting offers an advanced-degree experience waiver — Google's reqs convert it directly into a lower bar, and ScaleOps names a top-institution CS degree as a credential.

**De-emphasise:**

* **Freelance and teaching years as engineering tenure.** Keep teaching visible as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical explanation — it is a genuine strength and reads well in a hiring-manager conversation — but never let it blur the headline number. The honest claim is **~2–3 years of full-time professional software engineering**, and overstating it is the fastest way to lose credibility in a screen.
* **Frontend-first framing.** React/CSS/UI polish should support a backend-ownership story, not lead it. The one exception is Check Point and Comet, where a genuine full-stack balance is the ask.
* **Senior/lead vocabulary.** Do not claim architectural or organisational leadership. On the three roles with elevated expectations (MongoDB "led development work", SailPoint "high-throughput services securing millions of identities", ScaleOps 4+ yrs), compete on demonstrated end-to-end ownership of features and on debugging/root-cause depth — both are real and both are checkable.
* **Pure research or data-science positioning.** The ML/data-science research background is an asset *in service of* production AI engineering. Framed as research interest, it pushes the candidate toward roles this search deliberately excludes.

## 🔹 Search Gaps

What was actually searched, and what blocked me. All three markets were searched; the depth was uneven, and here is exactly how.

### Israel — searched comprehensively ✅

* **Indeed connector:** 7 searches across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ramat Gan, Petah Tikva, Ra'anana and Kfar Saba, on Go/Python/Kubernetes/AI-platform/full-stack keyword sets, plus `get_job_details` on 5 individual postings.
* **Company ATS board APIs, probed directly:** ~75 Greenhouse slugs, ~37 Ashby slugs and 9 Lever slugs for Israeli tech companies. **Hit rate was poor and that is the single biggest limitation of this run** — only 14 of ~75 Greenhouse slugs resolved (island, torq, riskified, lightricks, fireblocks, similarweb, jfrog, forter, melio, bringg, datarails, axonius, nice, cymulate) and only 4 of ~37 Ashby slugs (sentra, oligo, vanta, drata), of which **sentra and oligo returned a completely different company's postings** — an unrelated spacecraft-engineering org holds those board slugs. **Zero of 9 Lever slugs resolved.** Wiz, Cyera, Coralogix, Firebolt, Monday, HiBob, Gong, Snyk, Armis, Claroty, Orca, Cybereason, Transmit, Axonius-adjacent, SentinelOne, Checkmarx, Cymulate-adjacent, Silverfort, Descope, Frontegg, Port, Komodor, groundcover, Firefly and ~40 others were **not reachable by slug guessing** and are therefore **unsearched at the ATS level** this run. Their reqs are a real blind spot.
* **`boards-api.eu.greenhouse.io` was refused outright by the egress proxy** (`connect_rejected`, repeatedly). This blocked the Cymulate AI Full-Stack Engineer posting, which I surfaced from the board listing but **could not open to read requirements**, so it is excluded rather than graded. I worked around the host for ScaleOps by reading its board through the non-EU endpoint, but per-job EU fetches remained unavailable. **This is the highest-value fix for the next run.**
* **devjobs.co.il proved to be the most productive Israeli source** and is newly exploited this run. It renders under headless Chromium, publishes **years-of-experience on the listing card** (which makes seniority filtering cheap and reliable), and surfaced Comet, Upwind-adjacent leads, Lusha, Silverfort, Riverside, Alice and NielsenIQ. I scraped three grids (Backend, Full Stack, Software, 60 cards each). **I did not scrape its AI/ML, Cloud/DevOps or Data grids** — budget ran out, and those are likely to hold further matches. Recommend starting there next run.
* **Could not reach:** **Salesforce careers** — the "Mid Level Backend Software Engineer, Israel – Tel Aviv" req (jr313572) is a plausible strong match on title alone, but Akamai returned **Access Denied** to both WebFetch and headless Chromium, so it is unverified and excluded. **Zero Networks' Comeet per-role panel** rendered the company listing (confirming the role is live and labelled Mid-level) but not the requirements block, which is why #5 carries an explicit unconfirmed-requirements caveat. **alljobs.co.il and jobify360.co.il** returned many results via search but are Hebrew-language aggregators I did not fetch directly. **LinkedIn Jobs** was not fetchable, as expected.
* **Not searched at all:** Wellfound, Startup Nation Central, and Israeli VC portfolio boards (Aleph, TLV Partners, Glilot, Team8, Viola). These were deprioritised in favour of ATS-level verification, and they are the other main Israeli blind spot alongside the unresolved board slugs.

### New York City — searched actively, selectively included ✅

* **Dice:** NYC backend/Go/Kubernetes, last 7 days, direct-hire only — **7 results total**, and none clearing the gate: Atlassian's role is San Francisco, ATG's is Staff/remote, MSK's is bioinformatics, Capital One's is "Full-stack Engineer 4" at $215–245k (senior band), SecurityScorecard's is Distinguished, Censys' is Senior. **Dice is thin for mid-level NYC product engineering.**
* **ZipRecruiter:** NYC backend, MID+JUNIOR seniority filter, 14-day window, Go/K8s/Python skills — 5 of 280 returned. Nothing qualified: an application-support role, a software-engineering **internship**, Patreon's backend role at $189–255k (senior band), an Accenture specialist role, and Capital One's "Full-Stack Engineer 4 (Python, Kubernetes)". ZipRecruiter's seniority classifier does not reliably distinguish mid from senior, so these needed manual filtering anyway.

### Washington, DC — searched actively, one role included ✅

* **Indeed:** "Software Engineer backend Python Go / Washington, DC" returned only **two results**, neither usable: a **2027 graduate** electrical/computer engineering posting at JHU APL (Laurel, MD — excluded as a graduate role), and a Deloitte "Engineering Lead_Products & Platforms" in McLean at $141–278k (leadership band, excluded).
* **DC is structurally thin for this profile**, and the reason is worth stating plainly: a large share of DC-metro backend engineering sits behind **federal security-clearance or citizenship-plus-clearance requirements**. Clearance eligibility is **not** established by the Candidate Reference — U.S. citizenship is confirmed, clearance status is not — so any such req carries an unresolved eligibility risk and was excluded on that basis.
* **ID.me (McLean, VA) is the only DC-metro role that cleared the gate**, and it did so on requirements, not on scarcity: 3+ years, Go/Python/TypeScript, Kubernetes, DevEx/platform scope. Its risks are location-and-relocation and the AI-agent requirement bullets, both flagged in the table.
* **Not searched for DC:** Dice and ZipRecruiter were spent on NYC; DC-specific VC portfolio boards and DC startup career pages were not reached. Given the clearance structure of that market, I judged further DC breadth a poor use of remaining budget — but it is a real gap, not a conclusion.

### Honest summary of the U.S. result

Six U.S. roles were verified live and genuinely considered; **five are in the table and one (Pinecone Applied AI) plus Braze were excluded as Medium.** The five that made it did so because their published minimum requirements — "2+ years, Go or Python" (Robinhood), "at least 2 years" (Pinecone Experience), "3 years, compiled language, React/TypeScript/Kubernetes" (MongoDB), "TypeScript **and** production Go" (Vercel), "3+ years, Go/Python, DevEx/platform" (ID.me) — match the candidate on the mandatory lines rather than on transferable adjacency. Israel still dominates the table **9 to 6** (counting the Stretch at #15 among the Israel roles), which is the expected and intended shape.
