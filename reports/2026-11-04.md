## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / Platform Engineer — Go and Python, Kubernetes, high-throughput distributed services, with production generative-AI engineering.*

The three sentences that should open every application in this set:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100,000+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the strongest single line on the CV and it is what makes Google's control-plane req (#3), SailPoint's event-driven microservices req (#4), Appdome's internal-platform req (#1) and Writer's containerisation req (#11) all answerable. Lead with the *scale number*, not the job title.
2. **Production applications processing 100,000+ documents per day at Qualtrics.** This is the throughput proof that AppsFlyer (#12), Robinhood (#6) and Taboola (#15) are screening for.
3. **Built and evaluated production generative-AI systems — plus text classification, sentiment analysis and summarisation.** This is the *differentiator*, not a footnote. It is a stated mandatory requirement at Windward (#9), the reason Pinecone (#7) and Writer (#11) are credible at 2–3 years, and half of why AppsFlyer (#12) fits. Very few engineers at this experience level have it.

Add, where the stack calls for it: **React and TypeScript full-stack ownership** (#2, #7, #11, #15) — framed as *backend engineer who ships the UI too*, never as frontend capability.

**What to de-emphasise.** Freelance and vocational teaching years — valuable as evidence of communication and mentoring, and worth one line, but never positioned as engineering tenure; conflating them invites a credibility problem that is entirely avoidable. Drop the generic "full-stack developer" framing, which competes against a very deep pool and hides the Kubernetes and Go differentiators. Do not lead with academic machine-learning or data-science research: it pulls toward research-scientist screens the candidate does not want and is not positioned for. Finally, do not over-index on Java — it belongs on the skills list, but leading with it invites comparison against engineers with years of Spring depth (the real risk at #15 and the reason Capitolis, At-Bay and Yotpo were excluded).
