## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python and Kubernetes for high-scale cloud services, who also ships product full-stack and has built *and evaluated* production generative-AI systems.** Not a generic full-stack developer — that framing underperforms against every role ranked above #14 in this table.

1. **Kubernetes-controller development for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100k+ requests/day.** This sentence opens Check Point (both reqs — EKS is named explicitly in the Cloudinfra one), both Upwind reqs, Appdome, SailPoint, Robinhood, Writer, both Google reqs and AWS Transform. Tell it as a **design** decision, not a feature-delivery story: Google's SWE III rubric and AWS Transform's `2+ years of design or architecture` sub-requirement both reward that framing specifically.
2. **Go as a primary production language.** Still the single biggest differentiator in the Israeli mid-level market. It is a stated `Strong proficiency` requirement at SailPoint, a named backend language at Check Point and Robinhood, one of four accepted languages at Upwind and Plaid, and the compiled-language requirement at Pinecone. 3. **Having built *and evaluated* production generative-AI systems.** Central at monday.com and Writer, on-topic at Pinecone and Guidde, mandatory at Windward. Frame it as **AI-enabled product engineering and system evaluation** — never as research. The Upwind AI Engineer rejection above is the precise boundary: evaluating and shipping AI systems is the candidate's claim; training and fine-tuning models and benchmarking against the literature is not.

Pair these with the **MSc from UMD**, which is not decoration — it is the clause that clears the stated minimum qualification at both Google reqs.

**What to de-emphasize:**
* **Freelance teaching years.** They are strong evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — say so in one line — but never let them read as padding the professional engineering count. At 2–3 years of full-time SWE experience against reqs asking 2–4, the honest number is competitive; an inflated one invites a correction mid-interview.
* **Pure data-science, text-analytics and academic ML research framing.** It pulls toward the research reqs this report explicitly rejected (Upwind AI Engineer, Classiq) where the candidacy is weak. Translate it into *AI-system evaluation* and *high-throughput document processing* instead.
* **Java.** It is on the Indeed skills list and is worth exactly one bullet at Cato-style or AppsFlyer-style employers. Leading with it competes directly against engineers with 4+ years of Spring Boot and loses.
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake.** Five languages on a first screen reads as junior. Go, Python, TypeScript — then depth.
