## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.** Concretely, open every application with three artefacts, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at SAP, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the single most differentiating line on the CV and it is what Upwind, MongoDB, Papaya and Stigg are actually buying.
2. **Production generative-AI work — evaluating and improving GenAI systems, plus text analytics, classification and summarization over 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** In this market (ONE ZERO, Riskified, monday.com, Riverside, MongoDB Agent Engine) this converts a "mid-level backend engineer" into "mid-level backend engineer who has already shipped and measured AI in production" — a scarce combination at 2–3 years.
3. **React/TypeScript full-stack ownership at Qualtrics** — needed to clear the full-stack reqs (#3, #5, #8, #12, #13, #15) but it is the *supporting* claim, never the headline.

**De-emphasize:** the freelance teaching years as engineering tenure (present them as evidence of communication and mentoring, kept clearly separate from the ~2–3 years of professional SWE experience); academic ML/data-science research (it invites data-scientist screening, which is not the target); and any framing that reads as generic "full-stack developer", which competes against a very crowded Israeli field. Also avoid over-claiming seniority — several roles here are won by being the *strongest* candidate in a 2–3 year band rather than a marginal one in a 5+ band.
