## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.** In a week where almost every fresh Israeli req asks 4–6 years, the candidate's leverage comes from *depth of evidence at low years*, not from breadth.

Lead with, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at SAP, 100k+ requests/day.** The single most differentiating line available. It is what Upwind, MongoDB (all three reqs), Uber and Papaya are actually buying, and it is unusual at 2–3 years. Describe it as control-loop and reconciliation engineering, not as "DevOps work".
2. **Production generative-AI work — evaluating and improving GenAI systems, plus text analytics, classification and summarization over 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** For ONE ZERO, monday.com, GovEagle and MongoDB's Agent Engine this converts "mid-level backend engineer" into "mid-level backend engineer who has already shipped *and measured* AI in production" — a scarce combination in this band.
3. **Root-cause analysis and production debugging.** Uber names it as a preferred qualification and Papaya asks for it outright. Bring one concrete story with a measured before/after.
4. **MSc + BSc in CS, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA.** Use it as a differentiator in NYC, where it also unlocks the alumni network, and as credibility against senior-banded Israeli reqs.
5. **Bilingual Hebrew/English.** State it early on Israeli applications — it removes an unspoken question at companies like ONE ZERO.

**De-emphasize:**

- **Freelance/vocational teaching years.** Keep them as evidence of communication and mentoring, clearly separated from the ~2–3 years of full-time software engineering. Never let a reader total them together — it invites a seniority mismatch and then a downgrade.
- **Frontend-only framing.** Do not lead with React/CSS. Full-stack should always be presented as *backend ownership that extends into the UI*.
- **Academic ML/data-science research as a headline.** Useful supporting colour for Riskified and AI-platform roles; actively harmful if it makes the candidate read as a data-scientist applicant for a backend req.
- **Breadth-over-depth lists.** Against 4–6-year reqs, a long tool list reads as thin. Two deep systems stories beat ten technologies.
