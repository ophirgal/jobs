## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Position as a **Backend / Full-Stack Software Engineer specializing in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production generative-AI experience** — in that order. The evidence that actually moves these fifteen reqs, ranked by how often it appeared in a mandatory requirements list this run:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day.** This is the strongest single asset in the profile and it is mandatory-list material at #2, #9, #11 and #15. It is also *control-plane orchestration*, which is the exact phrase MongoDB's Atlas reqs use — say it that way.
2. **Go and Python as primary backend languages.** Named in the mandatory requirements of #1, #2, #4, #6, #7, #8, #9, #11, #12 and #15.
3. **Generative-AI system evaluation.** The differentiator at #4 (named must-have), #3, #6, #8, #12 and #13. Very few mid-level candidates can evidence *evaluation* as distinct from *using* LLM APIs. Lead with it wherever the posting mentions agents, LLMs, or MCP.
4. **Production root-cause analysis and debugging.** Explicitly a hiring criterion at #1 ("production debugging"), #3 ("you have debugged real production problems and can talk concretely about what broke and how you fixed it") and #8. Have two concrete, named incidents ready with the diagnosis path, not just the outcome.
5. **React/TypeScript full-stack from Qualtrics, plus 100k+ documents/day.** What makes the full-stack reqs (#2, #5, #10, #13) credible rather than aspirational.
6. **MSc + BSc in CS, University of Maryland, 3.9.** A hard requirement at #7 ("BSc in CS from a known university"), stated as an accepted alternative at #2 ("BSc or MSc"), and the lever for the level conversation at #10.

**What to de-emphasize:**
* **Freelance teaching years.** They are genuine evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — and every posting here values communication — but they are **not** full-time software engineering experience. Keep them as a short line under a "Teaching / Mentoring" heading, never folded into the engineering total. Against reqs that ask 3+ years, an inflated-looking total invites a recruiter to discount the whole CV.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** Relevant only as background. Every applied-AI req above wants production systems, not research; #4 says it outright ("professional or substantial personal work … with evidence that you have built something and learned where it breaks"). Do not let the research framing make the candidate look like a research-scientist applicant — that is the fastest route to the wrong rejection.
* **Breadth-for-breadth's-sake stack lists.** Naming Java, Scala, C# and others thinly is what makes a mid-level CV read as unfocused. Go, Python, TypeScript, Kubernetes, AWS, PostgreSQL — and depth on those.
* **Seniority language.** Nothing in this profile supports Senior, Staff or Lead framing, and reaching for it on a 3-year req reads as miscalibration.
