Lead with: **Backend engineer in Go and Python who builds and operates Kubernetes-based distributed services, and who has shipped and evaluated generative-AI systems in production.**

Three proof points should appear in the first six lines of every application:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day** — this is the single most differentiating item in the profile and it is mandatory or preferred in nine of the fifteen table roles.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** — the throughput-and-correctness story, and the right answer to any "scale" question.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — distinct from "I've used an LLM API," and the thing that separates this candidate at Pendo, Lemonade, monday.com, ONE ZERO, MongoDB Agent Engine and Writer.

Add **root-cause analysis and production debugging** as a fourth beat for Uber, Affirm and Madhive, all three of which name it explicitly.

**De-emphasise:**
* The **freelance teaching years** as engineering tenure. Keep teaching visible as evidence of communication and mentoring — Madhive, GovEagle and Pendo all weight stakeholder communication heavily — but never let it blur the **2–3 years of professional software engineering**, which is the number recruiters verify.
* **Academic ML and data-science research** as a headline. It is an asset *inside* a backend narrative (Lemonade, Pendo) and a liability in front of it — it invites a data-scientist read of the résumé, which is a role family the brief excludes.
* **Breadth-for-breadth's-sake stack listings.** "Generic full-stack developer" is the weakest available framing for this profile and the most competitive lane in both markets.
* **Frontend-first framing.** React and TypeScript are real and should be present — they are decisive at Madhive and Check Point — but as the second half of a backend story, never the first.
