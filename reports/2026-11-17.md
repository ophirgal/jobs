## Executive summary

**Three new roles cleared verification today, and the honest headline is that the Israeli mid-level market is unusually thin right now.** I swept Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and Comeet boards company-by-company and harvested the DevJobs backend/full-stack grid in full. The picture is consistent: Israel is currently advertising Senior, Staff, Principal and Team Lead almost exclusively. Of roughly 60 live Israeli backend/full-stack reqs I retrieved and read today, the overwhelming majority stated 5+, 7+ or 8+ years.

The strongest new find is **Ramp — Software Engineer, Fullstack, Spend Controls (NYC)**, published four days ago. It is Ramp's *unleveled* IC req — its sibling postings on the same board explicitly say "we're looking for a senior backend engineer," while this one says "we're looking for a fullstack engineer" and states no years minimum at all. Every bullet in its requirements list is experience-based and maps onto evidence in the profile, and Ramp's own postings name Python, TypeScript, React, Go and AWS as the house stack.

**Two roles left the table.** Zero Networks' Backend Engineer is confirmed closed — the position no longer appears on their Comeet board. Uber's Software Engineer II could not be re-verified through any retrievable source this run, so under the verification contract it is dropped rather than carried.

7. **MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9) plus teaching and mentoring.** Use it for the communication and technical-explanation requirements at #8, #11 and #16, and as the ML/AI credibility floor at #7 and #15.

**What to de-emphasize:**
- **Freelance teaching years as engineering tenure.** Present teaching as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical depth — never as professional software-engineering experience. Conflating them invites a credibility problem precisely where the candidate is already at the experience boundary.
- **Academic machine-learning and data-science research.** It reads as a signal toward data science or research-scientist tracks, which is the wrong lane. The exception is #7 and #15, where it is the floor under the AI-platform claim — mention it there in one line, as background, then return to engineering.
- **Breadth-for-its-own-sake stack lists.** Against 5+ year reqs, a long shallow list hurts. Lead with Go, Python and Kubernetes at depth.
- **Senior-sounding framing.** Do not reach for architecture-ownership or leadership language. Every High role here is explicitly hands-on IC; sounding senior at 2–3 years reads as miscalibration, not ambition.
