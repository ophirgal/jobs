## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specializing in Go and Python on Kubernetes, with production ownership of distributed, high-throughput services — and enough full-stack range in TypeScript/React to own a feature end to end.**

Three concrete proof points carry nearly every role in this table, and they should appear in the first six lines of any application:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — the strongest single asset. It is simultaneously *control-plane orchestration* (MongoDB Atlas), *cloud-native infrastructure built from scratch* (Appdome, Upwind), and *distributed SaaS at scale* (Cato, Check Point). Phrase it to match the posting's own vocabulary each time.
2. **A production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day** — this is the answer to every "high-throughput", "data at scale" and "async processing" bullet (Backslash, Appdome, ONE ZERO, Ramp).
3. **Generative-AI system building *and evaluation*** — note the emphasis. Many mid-level applicants can say they called an LLM API; very few can evidence *evaluating* AI systems. That distinction is the differentiator at ONE ZERO, monday.com, GovEagle, Appdome and Lemonade.

Add the **MSc plus 3.9 GPA** wherever an academic gate exists — it is a hard filter at NiCE and a tiebreaker elsewhere.

**De-emphasize:** freelance teaching years as though they were engineering tenure (keep the teaching, but frame it as mentoring and technical communication, which several of these reqs explicitly want); CSS and UI-implementation depth, which pulls toward the frontend roles this search excludes; academic ML and data-science research, which invites a research-scientist read rather than a software-engineering one; and any "generalist full-stack developer" framing — it is the weakest available positioning for this profile.

**Israel — searched comprehensively.** Covered: the DevJobs sitemap (67,581 job URLs), with the **1,400 most recent postings** enumerated and the newest **1,280** individually fetched and filtered by stated experience requirement; Greenhouse board APIs for 57 Israeli and Israel-hiring companies; SmartRecruiters (Check Point — all 239 live postings enumerated); Comeet-backed company careers pages (Upwind, Infinidat — all 28 postings enumerated, Coralogix); Ashby (18 organizations); the Indeed connector for Tel Aviv and Herzliya; plus targeted web search.

* **Comeet's positions API is effectively closed to me.** It requires a per-company token, and attempting to extract one from a page was blocked by this session's safety classifier — correctly so. The consequence is real: Comeet hosts a large share of Israeli startup hiring, and I can only reach those boards when the company's own careers page server-renders the job links (Upwind and Infinidat did; **Komodor, Port.io, Firefly, env0, Anecdotes, Zero Networks, Guidde and Stigg did not**). This is the single largest blind spot in the Israel search and it recurs every run.
* **Lever** returned no valid board for any of 30 Israeli companies tried — none of them use it.
* **Coralogix's own careers page** (`coralogix.com/careers/`) renders its position list client-side and showed only "Loading roles"; the individual Comeet posting was verifiable, the full board was not.
* **DevJobs `/jobs` listing and search pages returned HTTP 500 all run** (the per-posting pages and the sitemap worked). So DevJobs could only be mined by ID, not by query.
* **LinkedIn Jobs, Glassdoor and Startup Nation Central** were not directly fetchable, as in every prior run.
* **Two DevJobs listings proved stale when checked against the employer's own board** (Infinidat, Check Point DLP). This is now a confirmed, repeating failure mode for DevJobs — every DevJobs-sourced role in the table should ideally be corroborated at the employer, and the one that could not be (NiCE, #5) is labelled `[single-source]`.

**New York City — searched actively, 5 roles cleared the gate.** Covered: Ashby boards for Ramp (181 live postings), Madhive, GovEagle and Writer; Greenhouse APIs for Affirm, MongoDB and Pendo; the Indeed connector for New York, NY. Everything that cleared the strict U.S. gate is already in the table; nothing new surfaced today. Of the NYC results Indeed returned, the non-table entries were all senior (Senior/Staff Full Stack at Cloaked, TAXO, Ellipsis Labs, Josh Pros), domain-specialized, or not retrievable at a primary source.

**Washington, DC — searched actively, zero roles included.** Covered: the Indeed connector for Washington, DC (returning DC proper plus McLean, Arlington, Reston, Gaithersburg, Germantown and Annapolis Junction). **Why nothing cleared the gate:**

* The DC-metro engineering market returned by these searches is dominated by **cleared defense and government-services work**. EpochGeo's Geospatial Software Developer requires an active **TS/SCI**; Wyetech's Software Engineer Level 2 (Annapolis Junction) is a cleared NSA-adjacent req; SADOS Government Solutions' Mid-Level AI Engineer is a government-solutions role. The candidate is a U.S. citizen and needs no sponsorship, but **holding or being processable for an active TS/SCI is not established**, so these are unknown-eligibility risks, and the prompt's rule is to exclude rather than assume.
* The only large-brand DC result, **Palantir's Software Engineer**, was not a primary posting — see *Rejected on verification*.
* The remainder were off-target: a DNS Platform & DevOps req at Comcast (Reston, operations-weighted), a Ground Control Station new-grad req at SHIELD (Arlington), an embedded-systems req at Gaithersburg, and a $90–105k generalist role in Germantown.
* **Net:** DC produced no role that would plausibly be rated High for this candidate, and the gate says Medium and Stretch U.S. roles do not belong in the report. Zero is the correct answer, not a failure to search. **Dice and ZipRecruiter were not used this run** — the Indeed connector plus direct ATS enumeration had already established the shape of the DC market, and spending the remaining budget there was not justified.

**Not reached at all this run:** Wellfound, Workable-hosted boards, Workday-hosted boards, VC portfolio job boards (Israeli and U.S.), and Israeli tech-community boards. Each is a known source of mid-level backend roles and each is a real gap.
