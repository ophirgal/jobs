## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with:** *Backend / full-stack engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes on AWS, with production ownership of distributed services and hands-on generative-AI system work.*

Four concrete proof points, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway handling 100,000+ requests/day** — this is the headline. It is simultaneously a distributed-systems story, a Kubernetes story, a Go/Python story, and a control-plane story, and it is what makes Stigg, Upwind, MongoDB, Affirm and Cato all plausible from one résumé.
2. **Production applications processing 100,000+ documents/day** — the throughput and data-pipeline story. Use this for Braze, Riskified and Madhive.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems**, not merely calling model APIs — use the evaluation word explicitly. It is what separates this profile from the large pool of engineers who have "used LLMs," and it is a named requirement at Appdome, NiCE, Ramp AI Ventures, Riskified and GovEagle.
4. **Independently built internal engineering tools** — cite this whenever a req says "developer experience," "internal tooling," "initiative without a specification" or "ownership." It is the single most-underused line on this résumé and it is explicitly asked for by Stigg, NiCE and Ramp.

Keep **MSc + BSc in CS from UMD, both 3.9** visible at the top. It is a hard filter at NiCE (GPA ≥ 85), an explicit preference at Fullpath ("ideally top grades"), and a quiet differentiator everywhere else in a 2–3-year applicant pool.

**De-emphasise:**
- **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** They belong on the résumé as evidence of communication, mentoring and technical explanation — a single line, framed that way — and must never be allowed to read as software-engineering tenure. A recruiter who misreads them will either over-level the candidate into reqs they will not clear, or discount the whole timeline.
- **Academic ML and data-science research.** It invites classification as a data-science or research candidate, which is explicitly off-target. Mention it only when a req names evaluation, benchmarking or text analytics.
- **Breadth-for-breadth's-sake technology lists.** Against reqs like Stigg's and MongoDB's, three technologies described in depth beat fifteen listed.
- **Frontend-first framing.** React and TypeScript are real assets at Riskified, Braze, Madhive and Check Point — but they should always arrive *after* the backend story, never as the opening.
