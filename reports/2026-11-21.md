## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead as a **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python, Kubernetes and scalable cloud services, with production AI-system experience** — and in the U.S. market specifically, as a **mid-level engineer with 2–3 years who has already owned production systems at scale**, because the entire U.S. table turns on level credibility rather than on stack.

Three things should appear in the first two lines of every application:

1. **The two throughput numbers.** *Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100k+ requests/day* and *a production pipeline processing 100k+ documents/day*. These are the only facts on the profile that a 2–3-year engineer normally cannot offer, and they do the work of a seniority claim without making one. Every High role in this table — Plaid, Fullpath, Madhive, Stigg, MongoDB, GovEagle — is screening for exactly this.
2. **Generative-AI evaluation, not generative-AI enthusiasm.** Eleven of the sixteen reqs mention LLMs, agents or AI tooling, and almost every one asks for *evaluation, regression testing, guardrails or measured quality*. "I have built and evaluated production GenAI systems, including regression testing for output quality" separates the candidate from a very large field of applicants who have only used the APIs.
3. **Root-cause analysis as a named strength.** Flytrex, Madhive, Plaid, ControlMonkey and Affirm all centre on production correctness and debugging. Phrase it as an outcome, not a trait.

**De-emphasise, firmly:**

* **Teaching and TA years as engineering experience.** Head TA at UMD and current vocational instruction are genuine evidence of **communication, mentoring and technical depth** — deploy them for the mentoring lines in reqs like MongoDB's and in interviews about explaining trade-offs. Never let them read as padding the professional engineering count; a recruiter who spots inflation discounts everything else.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** It supports the AI-platform roles as context but invites mis-sorting into data-science or research pipelines, which are explicitly outside the target families.
* **Frontend breadth for its own sake.** React and TypeScript should always arrive attached to backend ownership — "full-stack with backend ownership," never "full-stack developer." HTML/CSS depth should not be foregrounded anywhere.
* **Breadth claims over depth.** With 2–3 years, a long technology list reads as shallow. Three systems described precisely beats fifteen technologies listed.
