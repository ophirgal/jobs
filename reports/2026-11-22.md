## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with full-stack delivery capability and hands-on generative-AI production experience.**

Three concrete proof points, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day at SAP** — this is the differentiating credential. Most applicants at 2–3 years have consumed Kubernetes; writing controllers for it is platform-engineering depth and is what makes ranks 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11 realistic.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics** — the throughput-and-scale story, and the bridge into data-heavy roles (ranks 1, 2).
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems, plus text analytics and classification** — now an explicit requirement at Fullpath, Appdome, Stigg and Riskified rather than a nice-to-have.

Add **MSc + BSc Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** wherever a degree is a stated requirement (Cato names it explicitly).

**De-emphasise:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** Keep them as one line evidencing mentoring and communication. Never let them read as engineering tenure — overstating seniority is the fastest way to be screened out of a 2–4 year req and to fail a 5+ year one anyway.
* **Pure frontend and CSS/UI work.** Frontend is supporting evidence for full-stack ownership, not the headline. Leading with React invites classification as a frontend engineer and forfeits the Go/Kubernetes differentiation.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** It is an asset for AI-platform roles but actively misleading for the backend roles that dominate this list — it suggests a research track rather than production engineering. Frame AI experience as *production systems and evaluation*, never as research.
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake stack lists.** Depth in Go/Python/Kubernetes beats a long inventory of technologies touched once.
