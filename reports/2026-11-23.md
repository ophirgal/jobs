## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend engineer specialising in Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with full-stack delivery capability and hands-on generative-AI production experience.**

Three proof points, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway serving 100k+ requests/day at SAP.** This is the differentiating credential and it is what makes ranks 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 13, 14 and 15 realistic at 2–3 years. Most applicants at this stage have *consumed* Kubernetes; writing controllers for it is platform-engineering depth.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day at Qualtrics.** The throughput-and-scale story, and the bridge into the data-heavy roles (ranks 1, 2, 16).
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems, plus text analytics, classification and summarisation.** This has now moved from nice-to-have to *mandatory* at AppsFlyer and Writer, and is an explicit requirement or advantage at Fullpath, Appdome, Check Point (×3) and MongoDB. Frame it as production systems and evaluation — never as research.

Add **MSc + BSc Computer Science, University of Maryland, 3.9 GPA** wherever a degree is a stated requirement. Cato and Check Point both name it explicitly ("BSc from a known university"), and it is a free point.

**De-emphasise:**
* **Freelance and vocational teaching years.** One line, as evidence of mentoring and communication. Never let them read as engineering tenure — overstating seniority is the fastest way to be screened out of a 2–4 year req and to fail a 5+ year one anyway.
* **Pure frontend and CSS/UI work.** Frontend is supporting evidence for full-stack ownership, not the headline. Leading with React invites classification as a frontend engineer and forfeits the Go/Kubernetes differentiation — note that the two Israeli full-stack roles rejected this run (ScaleOps, Lusha) were rejected *because* they were frontend-weighted, which is the classification to avoid.
* **Academic ML and data-science research.** An asset for AI-platform roles, actively misleading for the backend roles that dominate this list, because it suggests a research track rather than production engineering.
* **Breadth-for-its-own-sake stack lists.** Depth in Go/Python/Kubernetes beats a long inventory of technologies touched once.
