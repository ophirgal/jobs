## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **"Backend / Full-Stack Engineer — Go, Python and Kubernetes for scalable cloud services, with production AI-system experience."**

Three concrete proof points, in this order:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** (SAP) — the strongest single differentiator. It is infrastructure-grade backend work at a level most 2–3-year candidates cannot claim, and it is what makes the MongoDB, Stigg, Upwind and Check Point reqs credible.
2. **Production applications processing 100k+ documents/day** (Qualtrics) — high-throughput data-plane engineering; use this for Fullpath's CDP, Stigg's metering and Madhive's financial systems.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — frame as *engineering*, not research: evaluation harnesses, eval-driven iteration, hardening agents for production. This is the live differentiator for monday.com, MongoDB Atlas Agent Engine, Writer, AppsFlyer and Appdome.

Support with the **MSc in Computer Science (UMD, 3.9 GPA)** and root-cause-analysis ability, and use teaching experience strictly as evidence of **communication and mentoring** — never as engineering tenure.

**De-emphasize:**
- **Freelance/vocational teaching years as engineering experience.** The Indeed résumé lists a Vocational Training Instructor role as current, which risks reading as a career gap or as non-engineering seniority. Keep SAP first and most prominent; teaching belongs in a separate section.
- **Frontend-only framing.** CSS, design systems and UI implementation work should be present but subordinate — every High-probability role here is backend-weighted, and Ashley Digital's is explicitly 60–70% backend.
- **Pure ML/data-science research.** Academic ML research supports the AI-engineering story but should not position the candidate for MLOps or research roles, which is the exact mismatch that keeps Lemonade's ML Platform role off the ranked table.
- **Breadth-over-depth skill lists.** The Indeed résumé lists Java alongside Go and Python; leading with a long flat list dilutes the Go/Python/Kubernetes specialization that is the actual selling point.
