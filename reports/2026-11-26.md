## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / Platform Engineer specialising in Go and Python on Kubernetes, building high-throughput distributed services — with full-stack React/TypeScript delivery and production generative-AI experience.**

Three concrete proof points to put at the top of the CV and every outreach message:
1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway, 100k+ requests/day** — this is the differentiator. It simultaneously satisfies "Kubernetes," "distributed systems," "platform/infrastructure engineering" and "high-throughput services," which between them are mandatory items on Decagon, ElevenLabs, Writer, Connecteam, Robinhood and Stigg.
2. **Production systems processing 100k+ documents/day, with text analytics, classification, sentiment analysis and summarization** — the direct key to Genesys, and the credential that makes the AI-adjacent roles non-speculative.
3. **Improving and evaluating generative-AI systems** — frame as *engineering*, never research: evaluation harnesses, eval-driven iteration, hardening agents for production. This is the live differentiator for Genesys, Writer (both reqs), AppsFlyer and Komodor.

**De-emphasise:** freelance/vocational teaching as *experience* (keep it as evidence of communication and mentoring, and never let it blur the 2–3 year professional-engineering count); academic ML and data-science research, which invites mis-slotting into data-science or research tracks that are explicitly out of scope; frontend/CSS craft, which risks pulling the profile toward the pure-frontend roles being avoided; and any language framed as leadership or architecture ownership, which triggers senior-bar comparisons the experience cannot yet support.

## 🔹 Search Gaps — what was and was not reached

**Israel — searched comprehensively.** Confirmed reached: Greenhouse `boards-api` (30 boards returned data, including AppsFlyer, JFrog, Payoneer, Riskified, Similarweb, Taboola, Yotpo, Forter, Pagaya, Axonius, Island, Descope, GitLab), Ashby `posting-api` (41 boards, including Stigg), Check Point's SmartRecruiters feed (41 live Israel postings enumerated), Genesys's Workday CXS API, and the Indeed connector across Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Ramat Gan, Petah Tikva and Kfar Saba.

- **Comeet remains the single biggest Israeli blind spot.** Fullpath's Comeet-hosted page returned **HTTP 430** on two attempts (plain fetch and browser user-agent), Komodor's careers page returned **403**, and the Comeet careers API rejected the UID format (**HTTP 400**). This covers a meaningful slice of Israeli startups — Port, Firefly, env0, VAST Data, Team8 portfolio companies, OX Security, Noma, Dream, BioCatch. Fullpath survived in the table *only* because the Indeed connector independently confirmed it live; it is explicitly flagged `[unverified-source]`.
- **Lever produced zero Israeli boards.** The API is functional (control org returned HTTP 200) but none of ~75 Israeli-company slugs tried resolved — these companies are on Comeet or custom ATSes, not Lever. Not a tooling failure, but it means the Lever channel contributed nothing for Israel.
- **SmartRecruiters produced only one board** out of ~50 Israeli enterprise names tried (Check Point). NICE, Amdocs, Cellebrite, Verint, Mobileye, Playtika, Wix, monday.com and the Israeli banks did not resolve on that API.
- **Not reached this run:** Startup Nation Central, Israeli VC portfolio boards, Wellfound, and Israeli tech-community boards. Budget went to ATS APIs, which produced higher-confidence, fully-verifiable results. **LinkedIn Jobs** blocks automated fetch, as expected.
- **monday.com** could not be re-verified at all (JS-rendered careers page; Indeed re-search returned nothing) — hence its removal.

**New York City — searched actively; five roles cleared the strict gate** (Madhive, Plaid Full Stack, ElevenLabs, Writer ×2, Decagon — six including Decagon). Reached via the Ashby and Greenhouse APIs (60 NYC-eligible postings enumerated and filtered), plus the Indeed connector. Roles that **failed the gate and why**, to show the bar was actually applied:
- **Ramp** — 10 NYC backend reqs read in full; the AI Spend Management, Accounts Receivable and Accounting reqs all explicitly say "**we're looking for a senior backend engineer**," and AI Ventures wants "a **founder, founding engineer, or early engineer**." Excluded on seniority, not on fit.
- **Brex** — Software Engineer II, Backend requires "**4+ years**"; Data Enablement Platform requires "3+ years in a **data infra or data platform role**," which the candidate does not have. Both excluded.
- **Notion** — Growth requires "**10+ years**"; Web Infrastructure is a junior role but centred on browser/bundler/React performance, i.e. frontend, which is an excluded family.
- **Yotpo, Apple (Herzliya), AllCloud, ERGO NEXT** — all **5+ years**. **Bay AI** — 5+ years *and* mandatory Hebrew. **Payoneer VibeOps** — 4+ years plus mandatory Cloudflare Workers and heavy on-call/support. **Writer Infrastructure engineer** — 5+ years infra/DevOps. **Decagon Cloud Infrastructure** — DevOps/Terraform-weighted rather than software engineering. All excluded.

**Washington, DC — searched actively; one role cleared** (Remitly, Arlington, re-verified live via Dice, posted 2026-11-23 / modified 2026-11-25). Reached via Dice (613 full-time direct-hire DC-area results faceted), ZipRecruiter (788 results, junior/mid filtered), and the Indeed connector.

- **The honest finding, now consistent across runs:** the DC-metro engineering market in this window is dominated by **federal contracting roles requiring an active TS/SCI clearance** — Akina (multiple Python/Kubernetes/AI-infrastructure reqs, all "TS/SCI – Polygraph required"), Captivation Software, Shield Consulting, CACI (TS/SCI with Polygraph), Phoenix Operations Group, Esri (Backend Software Engineer – TS/SCI). Several of these are otherwise excellent technical matches on Python/Kubernetes/Docker/PostgreSQL. **Clearance eligibility cannot be assumed from the Candidate Reference, so all were excluded** under the operating rules, even where the stack fit was strong. This is the main structural reason DC yields ~1 role per run rather than several.
- Remaining DC-area roles were **senior or management** (Capital One Full-stack Engineer 4 "Manager IC", Comcast/FreeWheel Senior Manager Backend, CVS senior/staff reqs) or **recruiter/staffing listings** (StaffRight SDET, Eliassen DevOps, Gromelski, AttainX). Remitly's Arlington hub role remains the only genuine commercial-product DC-metro match, and it clears the gate on its own merits.
- **One correction worth flagging:** Dice now lists the Remitly role as **On-Site** in Arlington. Earlier runs described it as hybrid (~3 days). The work model should be confirmed with the recruiter rather than assumed.

**Connector performance, for future runs:** Indeed was productive for Israel (surfaced Genesys and Komodor, and independently rescued Fullpath) and near-useless for NYC/DC (3 and 1 results respectively). Dice was valuable for DC specifically and for re-verifying Remitly. ZipRecruiter added nothing that other sources had not already surfaced. The ATS APIs (Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday CXS) remain by far the highest-yield, highest-confidence channel and should keep getting the bulk of the budget.
