## 🔹 Positioning Recommendation

Lead with: **Backend / Full-Stack Engineer specialising in Go, Python, Kubernetes and high-throughput cloud services, with production generative-AI experience.**

Three concrete artefacts should carry the whole narrative, in this order:

1. **Kubernetes controllers for a globally distributed API gateway at 100,000+ requests/day** (SAP). This is the single most valuable line and it should be the first technical sentence in every application. It proves distributed-systems depth, Go, Kubernetes-at-the-controller-level rather than Kubernetes-as-a-user, and production scale — all at once. Note that it satisfied a named requirement in nine of the sixteen ranked roles.
2. **Production applications processing 100,000+ documents per day with text analytics, classification, sentiment analysis and summarization** (Qualtrics), plus **evaluating and improving generative-AI systems**. The evaluation experience is the rarer half and should be stated explicitly — most applicants to AI-adjacent backend roles have built with LLMs but cannot describe how they measured whether output got better. Genesys (#6), Lemonade (#15), Ramp AI Ventures (#10), Appdome (#5) and Connecteam (#3) all ask for it in some form.
3. **Root-cause analysis across distributed services, and independently built internal engineering tooling.** Ramp Accounting (#11) and Cyera (#16) state this almost word for word. Give a specific incident with a specific outcome rather than the generic claim.

**De-emphasise:** freelance teaching years — keep them as evidence of communication and mentoring, never as engineering tenure, and never let the total give an impression above 2–3 years of professional software engineering. Also de-emphasise academic machine-learning research when applying to product-engineering roles (it reads as a research-track signal and invites doubt about hands-on shipping); the exception is **Lemonade ML Platform (#15)**, where it is a genuine advantage. Do not foreground frontend or CSS work, and do not present the profile as a generic full-stack developer — the backend-plus-Kubernetes-plus-AI combination is the differentiator and a generic framing discards it.
